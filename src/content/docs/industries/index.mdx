Each industry guide explains how the RMF and SP 800-53 controls fit alongside that sector's own rules: which frameworks overlap, which controls carry extra weight, and what regulators and auditors expect to see. Federal civilian systems are covered by the [RMF section](/rmf/).

## Guides

<SectionIndex section="industries" heading="Guide">

No industry guide is published yet. Industry guides are planned for Phase 6 of the [roadmap](/reference/roadmap/#industries-and-technology-phase-6), and which industries come first is not decided. Each guide appears here when it is published, with a one-line summary.

</SectionIndex>

## Sectors a guide could cover

| Industry | Main rules alongside 800-53 |
| --- | --- |
| Defense and defense industrial base | DoDI 8510.01, CNSSI 1253, CMMC, SP 800-171 |
| Healthcare | HIPAA Security Rule, HHS 405(d) practices |
| Financial services | FFIEC guidance, GLBA Safeguards Rule, PCI DSS |
| Energy and utilities | NERC CIP, DOE C2M2 |
| State and local government | StateRAMP (GovRAMP), CJIS Security Policy, IRS Publication 1075 |

To write a new guide, copy the industry template from [Page templates](/reference/page-templates/) into this folder. Its front matter `description` is the one-line summary this page lists, and the build fails without one.
