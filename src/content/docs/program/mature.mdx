## Outcome

The program manages risk from its suppliers as deliberately as its own, and uses assessment results, incidents and exercises to improve its policies and priorities.

## Artifacts to produce

| Family | Artifacts | Main controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supply chain risk management | Supply Chain Risk Management Plan; supplier assessment questionnaire | SR-2, SR-6 |

## Decisions to make

- Which suppliers and products are critical enough to assess, and how deeply.
- Which lessons from assessments, incidents and exercises change the Risk Management Strategy or a family policy.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility in this stage |
| --- | --- |
| Chief Information Security Officer | Owns the supply chain plan and the program's improvement cycle |
| Procurement | Puts security requirements into contracts and runs supplier assessments |
| Senior leader | Reapproves the strategy when risk tolerance changes |

## RMF Prepare tasks this stage carries out

This stage adds no new organization-level Prepare task from [SP 800-37 Rev. 2](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/37/r2/final). It revisits the ones set up earlier: the risk management strategy (P-2) and organization risk assessment (P-3) as results come in, and the common controls (P-5) as services change. The mapping is the author's judgment.

## Done when

- [ ] Critical suppliers are identified and assessed.
- [ ] The Supply Chain Risk Management Plan is approved.
- [ ] The strategy and policies have each been reviewed against the last year's assessments and incidents.
