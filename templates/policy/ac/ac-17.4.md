title: Remote privileged commands and access
status: draft
stage: core
typical:
  ac-17.04_odp.01: emergency administration outside business hours and administration of cloud-hosted components
  ac-17.04_odp.02: investigation of security incidents
---

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall authorize the execution of privileged commands via remote access only for {{param:ac-17.04_odp.01}}, and only in a format that provides assessable evidence. (AC-17(4)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall authorize access to security-relevant information via remote access only for {{param:ac-17.04_odp.02}}, and only in a format that provides assessable evidence. (AC-17(4)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall document the rationale for such remote access in the system security plan. (AC-17(4)(b))
