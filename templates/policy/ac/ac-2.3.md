title: Disable accounts
status: draft
stage: core
typical:
  ac-02.03_odp.01: 1 business day
  ac-02.03_odp.02: 90 days
---

- The {{org:account-manager}} shall disable an account within {{param:ac-02.03_odp.01}} when the account has expired. (AC-2(3)(a))
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall disable an account within {{param:ac-02.03_odp.01}} when it is no longer associated with a user or individual. (AC-2(3)(b))
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall disable an account within {{param:ac-02.03_odp.01}} when it is in violation of organizational policy. (AC-2(3)(c))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system disables an account that has been inactive for {{param:ac-02.03_odp.02}}. (AC-2(3)(d))
