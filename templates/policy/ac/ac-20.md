  ac-20_odp.01: establish terms and conditions in a signed agreement with the organization that owns the external system

- Before authorized individuals access the system from an external system, or process, store or transmit organization-controlled information on one, the {{org:ciso}} shall {{param:ac-20_odp.01}}, consistent with the trust relationship with the organization that owns, operates or maintains the external system. (AC-20a)
- Users shall not use {{param:ac-20_odp.04}} to access the system or to process, store or transmit organization-controlled information. (AC-20b)
