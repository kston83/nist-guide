- The {{org:system-owner}} shall designate the individuals authorized to make information publicly accessible. (AC-22a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure those individuals are trained to keep nonpublic information out of publicly accessible content. (AC-22b)
- The designated individuals shall review proposed content for nonpublic information before posting it on the publicly accessible system. (AC-22c)
- The designated individuals shall review the content on the publicly accessible system for nonpublic information {{param:ac-22_odp}}. (AC-22d)
- The designated individuals shall remove nonpublic information when a review discovers it. (AC-22d)
