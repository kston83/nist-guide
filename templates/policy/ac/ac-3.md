:::guidance
AC-3 is enforced by the system, not by people: the statement makes the system owner accountable for configuring it. Assessors check that the roles and permissions in the system match the access authorizations approved under AC-2.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system enforces approved authorizations for logical access to information and system resources in accordance with this policy. (AC-3)
