  ac-06.07_odp.01: quarterly for privileged roles and at least annually for all other roles

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review {{param:ac-06.07_odp.01}} the privileges assigned to {{param:ac-06.07_odp.02}} to validate the need for them. (AC-6(7)(a))
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall reassign or remove privileges the review finds are not needed for the user's mission and business functions. (AC-6(7)(b))
