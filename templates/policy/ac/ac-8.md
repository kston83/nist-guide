:::guidance
The banner text is usually approved once by legal counsel and reused on every system. The four notices listed in AC-8a are written for U.S. Government systems; the statements below keep the ones that apply to any organization and move the two government-only notices to the federal section.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system displays {{param:ac-08_odp.01}} to users before granting access. (AC-8a)
- The notice shall state that system usage may be monitored, recorded and subject to audit; that unauthorized use of the system is prohibited; and that use of the system indicates consent to monitoring and recording. (AC-8a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the notice remains on the screen until users acknowledge the usage conditions and take explicit action to log on to or further access the system. (AC-8b)
- For publicly accessible systems, the {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system displays system use information {{param:ac-08_odp.02}}, before granting further access. (AC-8c.1)
- For publicly accessible systems, the {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure any references to monitoring, recording or auditing are consistent with the privacy accommodations for such systems. (AC-8c.2)
- For publicly accessible systems, the {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the notice describes the authorized uses of the system. (AC-8c.3)

:::federal
SP 800-53 Rev. 5 AC-8a requires the notice on federal systems to state two more things ([AC-8](/controls/ac/ac-8/)).

- The notice shall state that users are accessing a U.S. Government system. (AC-8a.1)
- The notice shall state that unauthorized use of the system is subject to criminal and civil penalties. (AC-8a.3)

:::
