:::guidance
Teach users what potential insider threat indicators look like, such as attempts to get access beyond one's job, unexplained removal of information, or ignoring security rules repeatedly, and where to report them. Keep the reporting channel the same one the insider threat program (PM-12) uses.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall include in literacy training how to recognize and report potential indicators of insider threat. (AT-2(2))
