status: draft

:::guidance
Social engineering tricks people into giving up information or access, by email, phone, text or in person; social mining gathers information about the organization from public sources and social media to plan an attack. Phishing simulations (AT-2b) are a common way to practice recognizing it.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall include in literacy training how to recognize and report potential and actual instances of social engineering and social mining. (AT-2(3))
