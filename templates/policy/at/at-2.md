:::guidance
Literacy training is the course every user takes; awareness is what keeps the message alive between courses. Assessors compare completion records with account creation dates, so require the initial course before access (AC-2) and track it for employees, contractors and executives alike. The [training plan](/templates/plans/security-and-privacy-training-plan/) sets out the courses and schedule.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall provide security literacy training to all system users, including managers, senior executives and contractors, as part of initial training before they are given access, and {{param:at-02_odp.01}} thereafter. (AT-2a.1)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall provide privacy literacy training to all system users, including managers, senior executives and contractors, as part of initial training before they are given access, and {{param:at-02_odp.02}} thereafter. (AT-2a.1)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall provide security literacy training when system changes require it, and following {{param:at-02_odp.03}}. (AT-2a.2)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall provide privacy literacy training when system changes require it, and following {{param:at-02_odp.04}}. (AT-2a.2)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall increase users' security and privacy awareness through {{param:at-02_odp.05}}. (AT-2b)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall update literacy training and awareness content {{param:at-02_odp.06}} and following {{param:at-02_odp.07}}. (AT-2c)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall incorporate lessons learned from internal and external security incidents and breaches into literacy training and awareness techniques. (AT-2d)

:::federal
[5 CFR 930.301](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-930/subpart-C/section-930.301) (OPM) requires that all users of federal information systems be exposed to security awareness materials at least annually, and that new employees receive awareness material before they are allowed access. [44 U.S.C. § 3554(b)(4)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3554&num=0&edition=prelim) requires security awareness training for personnel, including contractors and other users. [OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf) (July 28, 2016), Appendix II, section 5.g, requires mandatory agency-wide privacy awareness and training for all employees and contractors. Checked September 2026.

- The {{org:ciso}} shall give new employees security awareness material before they are allowed access to federal information systems. (AT-2a.1)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall expose every user of federal information systems, including contractors, to security awareness materials at least annually. (AT-2a.1)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall maintain mandatory privacy awareness and training for all employees and contractors, as OMB Circular A-130 Appendix II requires. (AT-2a.1)

:::
