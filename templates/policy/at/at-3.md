:::guidance
Role-based training goes beyond what every user learns: administrators learn secure configuration, developers secure coding, authorizing officials how to weigh risk. List each role and its courses in the [training plan](/templates/plans/security-and-privacy-training-plan/). The NICE Workforce Framework for Cybersecurity ([SP 800-181 Rev. 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/181/r1/final), with its [components](https://www.nist.gov/itl/applied-cybersecurity/nice/nice-framework-resource-center/nice-framework-current-versions) at v2.2.0 as of September 2026) describes work roles and the knowledge and skills each needs, a useful starting point for the course list. Incident response training is covered by IR-2 and contingency training by CP-3.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall provide role-based security training to personnel with these roles and responsibilities: {{param:at-03_odp.01}}. (AT-3a)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall provide role-based privacy training to personnel with these roles and responsibilities: {{param:at-03_odp.02}}. (AT-3a)
- Role-based training shall be completed before the person is authorized to access the system or information, or to perform the assigned duties, and {{param:at-03_odp.03}} thereafter. (AT-3a.1)
- Role-based training shall also be provided when system changes require it. (AT-3a.2)
- The {{org:ciso}} and the {{org:privacy-official}} shall update role-based training content {{param:at-03_odp.04}} and following {{param:at-03_odp.05}}. (AT-3b)
- The {{org:ciso}} and the {{org:privacy-official}} shall incorporate lessons learned from internal and external security incidents and breaches into role-based training. (AT-3c)

:::federal
[5 CFR 930.301](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-930/subpart-C/section-930.301) (OPM) requires agencies to identify employees with significant information security responsibilities and give them role-specific training in accordance with NIST standards and guidance; refresher training as often as the agency determines necessary, based on the sensitivity of the information; and training when the system environment or procedures change significantly, or when an employee enters a position that needs more role-specific training. [OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf), Appendix II, section 5.g, requires role-based privacy training, before access or assigned duties, for employees and contractors with privacy roles, including managers. Checked September 2026.

- The {{org:ciso}} shall identify employees with significant information security responsibilities and give them role-specific training in accordance with NIST standards and guidance. (AT-3a)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall provide role-specific training when an employee enters a position that requires more of it, and when the system environment or procedures change significantly. (AT-3a.2)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall provide role-based privacy training to employees and contractors with privacy roles, including managers, before they are authorized to access federal information or systems or to perform their duties. (AT-3a.1)

:::
