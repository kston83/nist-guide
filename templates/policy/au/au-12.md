- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure {{param:au-12_odp.01}} provide an audit record generation capability for the event types identified under AU-2a. (AU-12a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall allow only {{param:au-12_odp.02}} to select the event types logged by specific components of the system. (AU-12b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system generates audit records for the event types specified under AU-2c, with the content required by AU-3. (AU-12c)
