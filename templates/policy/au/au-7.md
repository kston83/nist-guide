---
control: au-7
title: Audit record reduction and report generation
status: draft
stage: core
---

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall provide and implement an audit record reduction and report generation capability that supports on-demand review, analysis and reporting and after-the-fact investigation of incidents. (AU-7a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure that capability does not alter the original content or time ordering of audit records. (AU-7b)
