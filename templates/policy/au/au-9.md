- The {{org:system-owner}} shall protect audit information and audit logging tools from unauthorized access, modification and deletion. (AU-9a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure {{param:au-09_odp}} are alerted when unauthorized access, modification or deletion of audit information is detected. (AU-9b)
