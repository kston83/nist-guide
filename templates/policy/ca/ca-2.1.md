control: ca-2.1
title: 'Independent assessors'
status: draft
stage: operate
---

:::guidance
An independent assessor has no actual or perceived conflict of interest with the system: they do not build, run or manage it, do not assess their own work, and do not advocate for the organization they assess. Independence can come from a separate internal team or a third-party assessor, provided the system owner cannot influence the assessor's work or findings. In a small organization where every possible assessor is in the system owner's chain, NIST's CA-2(1) discussion allows an independent team of experts to review and validate the assessment results instead.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall employ independent assessors or assessment teams to conduct control assessments. (CA-2(1))
- The assessor shall be free of any actual or perceived conflict of interest regarding the development, operation, sustainment or management of the system under assessment. (CA-2(1))
- The authorizing official shall determine the level of assessor independence the system needs, based on its security category and risk, and confirm it before approving the assessment plan. (CA-2(1))
