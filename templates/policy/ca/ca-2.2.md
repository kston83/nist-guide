:::guidance
Specialized assessments go beyond the standard examine, interview and test methods to exercise the organization's defenses as an adversary or insider would. NIST's CA-2(2) discussion has the authorizing official approve the methods, in coordination with the organization's risk executive function. Unannounced assessments test detection and response more realistically but need tighter rules of engagement, so organizations often start with announced ones.
:::

- The assessor shall include {{param:ca-02.02_odp.02}} {{param:ca-02.02_odp.03}} as part of control assessments, {{param:ca-02.02_odp.01}}. (CA-2(2))
- The authorizing official shall approve the specialized assessment methods, and their rules of engagement, before they are used. (CA-2(2))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall enter each vulnerability that a specialized assessment finds into the system's vulnerability remediation process (RA-5). (CA-2(2))
