:::guidance
The plan of action and milestones (POA&M) records every known weakness until it is fixed or formally accepted. It connects assessments to remediation, and assessors read it closely because it shows whether the organization acts on what it finds. The [POA&M template](/templates/forms/plan-of-action-and-milestones/) lists the fields. Weaknesses in inherited common controls belong on the POA&M of the organization that provides them.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall develop a plan of action and milestones for the system that documents the planned remediation actions to correct weaknesses or deficiencies noted during control assessments and to reduce or eliminate known vulnerabilities in the system. (CA-5a)
- Each item in the plan of action and milestones shall record the weakness and its source, the responsible person or team, the resources required, the milestones and the scheduled completion date. (CA-5a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall update the plan of action and milestones {{param:ca-05_odp}}, based on the findings from control assessments, independent audits or reviews, and continuous monitoring activities. (CA-5b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall record the reason for each change to a scheduled completion date, and close an item only with evidence that the weakness is corrected. (CA-5b)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall review items past their scheduled completion date each month, and report them to the authorizing official. (CA-5b)

:::guidance
A weakness the organization decides not to fix goes through risk acceptance (RA-7), within the tolerance the risk management strategy sets. Record the acceptance and its review date against the item, so an assessor can trace every finding to either a fix or a decision.
:::

:::federal
The Federal Information Security Modernization Act requires each agency's information security program to include "a process for planning, implementing, evaluating, and documenting remedial action to address any deficiencies in the information security policies, procedures, and practices of the agency" ([44 U.S.C. § 3554(b)(6)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3554&num=0&edition=prelim)). [OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf), Appendix I, section 4.c(15), requires agencies to use POA&Ms to record and manage the mitigation and remediation of identified weaknesses and deficiencies not associated with accepted risks, and to make them available to OMB, DHS, inspectors general and the U.S. Government Accountability Office on request. Section 4.k requires agencies to correct deficiencies identified through assessments, continuous monitoring and audits, and to track and manage their remediation through the POA&M process, with material deficiencies also reported in the annual Federal Managers Financial Integrity Act report. As of September 2026.

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall record in the plan of action and milestones each weakness and deficiency not associated with an accepted risk, including those found by OMB reviews and internal or external audits, as OMB Circular A-130, Appendix I, sections 4.c(15) and 4.k, requires. (CA-5a)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall make plans of action and milestones available to OMB, DHS, the inspector general and the U.S. Government Accountability Office on request, as OMB Circular A-130, Appendix I, section 4.c(15), requires. (CA-5b)

:::
