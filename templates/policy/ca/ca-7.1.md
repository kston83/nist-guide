:::guidance
The independence rules of CA-2(1) apply to ongoing assessments too. Automated monitoring data can come from the system's own tools, but the people who assess what the data shows should not be the system's administrators. Common ways to meet it are a continuous monitoring team in the security office or the same third-party assessor that performs the yearly assessment.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall employ independent assessors or assessment teams to monitor the controls in the system on an ongoing basis. (CA-7(1))
- The ongoing assessors shall meet the independence the authorizing official set for the system under CA-2(1). (CA-7(1))
