:::guidance
Risk monitoring ties continuous monitoring to the risk tolerance in the risk management strategy (PM-9). Effectiveness monitoring asks whether the risk responses work, compliance monitoring asks whether they are in place, and change monitoring looks for changes that could alter the risk. Change monitoring usually draws on configuration change control (CM-3), security impact analyses (CM-4) and the component inventory (CM-8).
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall make risk monitoring an integral part of the system's continuous monitoring strategy, informed by the organization's risk tolerance. (CA-7(4))
- The strategy shall include effectiveness monitoring, to determine whether the implemented risk response measures remain effective. (CA-7(4)(a))
- The strategy shall include compliance monitoring, to verify that required risk response measures are implemented and that security and privacy requirements are satisfied. (CA-7(4)(b))
- The strategy shall include change monitoring, to identify changes to the system and its environment of operation that may affect security and privacy risk. (CA-7(4)(c))
