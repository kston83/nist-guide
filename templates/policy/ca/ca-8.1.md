:::guidance
An independent penetration testing team is one with no conflict of interest in the system, as for independent assessors under CA-2(1). It can be an internal red team outside the system's chain of command or an outside firm.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall employ an independent penetration testing agent or team to perform penetration testing on the system or system components. (CA-8(1))
