---
control: cm-10
title: Software usage restrictions
status: draft
stage: core
---

- Users shall use software and its documentation in accordance with contract agreements and copyright laws. (CM-10a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall track the use of software and documentation protected by quantity licenses to control copying and distribution. (CM-10b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall control and document the use of peer-to-peer file sharing technology so that it is not used for unauthorized distribution, display, performance or reproduction of copyrighted work. (CM-10c)
