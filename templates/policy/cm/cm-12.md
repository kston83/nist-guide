- The {{org:system-owner}} shall identify and document the location of {{param:cm-12_odp}} and the specific components on which it is processed and stored. (CM-12a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall identify and document the users who have access to the system and components where that information is processed and stored. (CM-12b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall document changes to the location where that information is processed and stored. (CM-12c)
