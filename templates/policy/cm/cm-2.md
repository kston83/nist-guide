title: Baseline configuration
status: draft
stage: core
typical:
  cm-02_odp.01: at least annually
  cm-02_odp.02: 'a significant change to the system, a new version of the secure configuration it is based on, or a security incident'
---

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall develop, document and maintain under configuration control a current baseline configuration of the system. (CM-2a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review and update the baseline configuration {{param:cm-02_odp.01}}. (CM-2b.1)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review and update the baseline configuration when required by {{param:cm-02_odp.02}}. (CM-2b.2)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review and update the baseline configuration when system components are installed or upgraded. (CM-2b.3)
