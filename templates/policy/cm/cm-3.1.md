title: 'Automated documentation, notification and prohibition of changes'
status: draft
stage: core
typical:
  cm-03.01_odp.01: the change management system integrated with the deployment pipeline
  cm-03.01_odp.02: the change control board members
  cm-03.01_odp.03: 5 business days
  cm-03.01_odp.04: 'the requester, the system owner and the security operations team'
---

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use {{param:cm-03.01_odp.01}} to document proposed changes to the system. (CM-3(1)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use those mechanisms to notify {{param:cm-03.01_odp.02}} of proposed changes and request approval. (CM-3(1)(b))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use those mechanisms to highlight proposed changes not approved or disapproved within {{param:cm-03.01_odp.03}}. (CM-3(1)(c))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use those mechanisms to prohibit changes until the designated approvals are received. (CM-3(1)(d))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use those mechanisms to document all changes to the system. (CM-3(1)(e))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use those mechanisms to notify {{param:cm-03.01_odp.04}} when approved changes are completed. (CM-3(1)(f))
