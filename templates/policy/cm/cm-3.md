:::guidance
CM-3 is where most programs either show discipline or show a gap. Keep one change record per change, with the request, the impact analysis (CM-4), the decision, the test result and the implementation date. Assessors sample changes from the system and trace each back to an approved record.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall determine and document the types of changes to the system that are configuration-controlled. (CM-3a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review proposed configuration-controlled changes and approve or disapprove them with explicit consideration of security and privacy impact analyses. (CM-3b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall document configuration change decisions for the system. (CM-3c)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall implement only approved configuration-controlled changes. (CM-3d)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall retain records of configuration-controlled changes for {{param:cm-03_odp.01}}. (CM-3e)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall monitor and review activities associated with configuration-controlled changes. (CM-3f)
- Configuration change control activities shall be coordinated and overseen by {{param:cm-03_odp.02}}, which convenes {{param:cm-03_odp.03}}. (CM-3g)
