- The {{org:system-owner}} shall identify {{param:cm-07.05_odp.01}} as the software programs authorized to execute on the system. (CM-7(5)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall employ a deny-all, permit-by-exception policy to allow only authorized software programs to execute. (CM-7(5)(b))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review and update the list of authorized software programs {{param:cm-07.05_odp.02}}. (CM-7(5)(c))
