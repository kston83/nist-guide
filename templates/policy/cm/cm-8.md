  cm-08_odp.01: 'name, type, manufacturer and model, serial number or asset tag, software version, location, network address, owner and administrator'
  cm-08_odp.02: at least quarterly

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall develop and document an inventory of system components that accurately reflects the system and includes all its components. (CM-8a.1, a.2)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the inventory has no duplicate accounting of components and no components assigned to another system. (CM-8a.3)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the inventory is at the level of granularity needed for tracking and reporting. (CM-8a.4)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the inventory includes {{param:cm-08_odp.01}} for each component. (CM-8a.5)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review and update the inventory {{param:cm-08_odp.02}}. (CM-8b)
