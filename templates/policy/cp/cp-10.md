status: draft

  cp-10_odp.01: 'the recovery time objective set in the contingency plan from the business impact analysis'
  cp-10_odp.02: 'the reconstitution time set in the contingency plan'
---

:::guidance
Recovery brings the system back into operation; reconstitution returns it to its fully operational, secure state, with its controls working and checked. After a compromise, recover from backups known to be clean.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall provide for the recovery of the system to a known state within {{param:cp-10_odp.01}} after a disruption, compromise or failure. (CP-10)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall provide for the reconstitution of the system to a known state within {{param:cp-10_odp.02}} after a disruption, compromise or failure. (CP-10)
