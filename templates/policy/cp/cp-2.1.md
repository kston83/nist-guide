:::guidance
Related plans include the business continuity plan, the continuity of operations plan, the disaster recovery plan, the incident response plan and the crisis communications plan. Coordination means the plans agree on priorities, recovery times, contacts and who declares what.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall coordinate contingency plan development with the organizational elements responsible for related plans. (CP-2(1))
