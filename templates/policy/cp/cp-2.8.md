:::guidance
Critical assets are the components, services and people the functions cannot run without. The criticality analysis (RA-9) and the business impact analysis are the usual sources.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall identify the critical system assets that support {{param:cp-02.08_odp}} mission and business functions. (CP-2(8))
