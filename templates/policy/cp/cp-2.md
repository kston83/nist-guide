:::guidance
The contingency plan says how a system is kept going, or brought back, after a disruption, compromise or failure. Base it on a business impact analysis, which sets the recovery objectives. NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1 describes the planning process and a plan outline.
:::

- Each {{org:system-owner}} shall develop a contingency plan for the system that identifies essential mission and business functions and the contingency requirements that go with them. (CP-2a.1)
- The contingency plan shall provide recovery objectives, restoration priorities and metrics. (CP-2a.2)
- The contingency plan shall set out contingency roles and responsibilities, and the assigned individuals with their contact information. (CP-2a.3)
- The contingency plan shall address maintaining essential mission and business functions despite a system disruption, compromise or failure. (CP-2a.4)
- The contingency plan shall address eventual, full system restoration without deterioration of the controls originally planned and implemented. (CP-2a.5)
- The contingency plan shall address the sharing of contingency information. (CP-2a.6)
- The contingency plan shall be reviewed by {{param:cp-02_odp.01}} and approved by {{param:cp-02_odp.02}}. (CP-2a.7)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall distribute copies of the contingency plan to {{param:cp-02_odp.03}} and to {{param:cp-02_odp.04}}. (CP-2b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall coordinate contingency planning activities with incident handling activities. (CP-2c)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review the contingency plan {{param:cp-02_odp.05}}. (CP-2d)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall update the contingency plan to address changes to the organization, the system or its environment of operation, and problems found when the plan is carried out or tested. (CP-2e)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall communicate contingency plan changes to {{param:cp-02_odp.06}} and to {{param:cp-02_odp.07}}. (CP-2f)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall incorporate lessons learned from contingency plan testing, training and actual contingency activities into contingency testing and training. (CP-2g)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall protect the contingency plan from unauthorized disclosure and modification. (CP-2h)
