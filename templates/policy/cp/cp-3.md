:::guidance
Contingency training teaches people with a role in the plan what they do and how: who declares a disruption, how to restore from backup, how to fail over to the alternate site. Users without a contingency role need only know where to get instructions, which literacy training (AT-2) can cover.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall provide contingency training, consistent with assigned roles and responsibilities, to system users within {{param:cp-03_odp.01}} of their assuming a contingency role or responsibility. (CP-3a.1)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall provide that training when system changes require it. (CP-3a.2)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall provide that training {{param:cp-03_odp.02}} thereafter. (CP-3a.3)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review and update contingency training content {{param:cp-03_odp.03}} and following {{param:cp-03_odp.04}}. (CP-3b)
