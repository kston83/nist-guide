title: 'Contingency plan testing'
status: draft
stage: operate
typical:
  cp-04_odp.01: 'annually'
  cp-04_odp.02: 'a functional test that restores the system, or a representative part of it, from backup'
  cp-04_odp.03: 'a tabletop exercise that walks the contingency team through the plan'
---

:::guidance
A tabletop exercise checks that people know the plan; a functional test checks that recovery actually works. Record each test in a test plan and an after-action report, and track corrective actions to closure.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall test the contingency plan {{param:cp-04_odp.01}}, using {{param:cp-04_odp.02}} to determine the plan's effectiveness and {{param:cp-04_odp.03}} to determine readiness to execute it. (CP-4a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review the contingency plan test results. (CP-4b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall initiate corrective actions where the results show they are needed, and track them in the plan of action and milestones. (CP-4c)
