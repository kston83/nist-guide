- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure primary and alternate telecommunications service agreements contain priority-of-service provisions in accordance with the system's availability requirements, including recovery time objectives. (CP-8(1)(a))
- Where the primary or alternate telecommunications services are provided by a common carrier, the {{org:system-owner}} shall request Telecommunications Service Priority for all telecommunications services used for national security emergency preparedness. (CP-8(1)(b))

:::federal
[Telecommunications Service Priority](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/services/telecommunications-service-priority-tsp) is an FCC program, managed by CISA, which requires service providers to prioritize provisioning and restoration of voice and data circuits for organizations with national security and emergency preparedness missions (checked September 2026). CISA's priority services are open to all levels of government and to organizations in the critical infrastructure sectors.

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall register the system's national security and emergency preparedness circuits in the Telecommunications Service Priority program. (CP-8(1)(b))

:::
