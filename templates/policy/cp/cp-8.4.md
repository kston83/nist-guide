- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require primary and alternate telecommunications service providers to have contingency plans. (CP-8(4)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review provider contingency plans to ensure they meet the organization's contingency requirements. (CP-8(4)(b))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall obtain evidence of contingency testing by providers {{param:cp-08.04_odp.01}}, and of contingency training by providers {{param:cp-08.04_odp.02}}. (CP-8(4)(c))
