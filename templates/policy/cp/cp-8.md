:::guidance
Alternate telecommunications keep the system reachable when the primary network or internet service fails, at the primary site or at the alternate sites: for example a second internet provider, a wireless backup link, or a provider-managed network with diverse paths.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall establish alternate telecommunications services, including the agreements needed to permit the resumption of {{param:cp-08_odp.01}} for essential mission and business functions within {{param:cp-08_odp.02}} when the primary telecommunications capabilities are unavailable at the primary or alternate processing or storage sites. (CP-8)
