---
control: ia-12
title: Identity proofing
status: draft
stage: core
---

- The {{org:account-manager}} shall identity proof users who require accounts for logical access, at the identity assurance level the applicable standards require, such as the NIST SP 800-63-4 digital identity guidelines. (IA-12a)
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall resolve each user identity to a unique individual. (IA-12b)
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall collect, validate and verify identity evidence. (IA-12c)
