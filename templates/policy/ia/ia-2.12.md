title: Acceptance of PIV credentials
status: draft

:::guidance
Personal Identity Verification (PIV) is the U.S. federal smart card credential. An organization outside the federal government meets this enhancement where it relies on PIV or PIV-interoperable credentials; if it uses none, record the enhancement as not applicable in the system security plan with that reason.
:::

- Where the organization issues or relies on Personal Identity Verification (PIV)-compliant credentials, the {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system accepts and electronically verifies them. (IA-2(12))

:::federal
Federal employees and contractors are issued PIV credentials under Homeland Security Presidential Directive 12 (HSPD-12) and [FIPS 201-3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/201-3/final), Personal Identity Verification (PIV) of Federal Employees and Contractors (January 2022).

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system accepts and electronically verifies PIV credentials for organizational users. (IA-2(12))

:::
