- The {{org:account-manager}} shall assign an individual, group, role, service or device identifier only with authorization from {{param:ia-04_odp.01}}. (IA-4a)
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall select an identifier that identifies the individual, group, role, service or device. (IA-4b)
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall assign the identifier to the intended individual, group, role, service or device. (IA-4c)
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall prevent reuse of identifiers for {{param:ia-04_odp.02}}. (IA-4d)
