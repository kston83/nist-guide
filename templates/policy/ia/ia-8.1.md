control: ia-8.1
title: Acceptance of PIV credentials from other agencies
status: draft
stage: core
---

:::guidance
This enhancement applies where federal employees or contractors from other agencies use the system. Outside that case, record it as not applicable in the system security plan with that reason.
:::

- Where users from federal agencies access the system, the {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure it accepts and electronically verifies their Personal Identity Verification (PIV)-compliant credentials. (IA-8(1))
