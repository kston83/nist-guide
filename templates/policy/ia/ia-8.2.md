---
control: ia-8.2
title: Acceptance of external authenticators
status: draft
stage: core
---

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system accepts only external authenticators that are NIST-compliant, such as those meeting [NIST SP 800-63B-4](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/63/b/4/final) (July 2025). (IA-8(2)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall document and maintain a list of accepted external authenticators. (IA-8(2)(b))
