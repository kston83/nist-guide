- The {{org:incident-response-team}} shall provide incident response training, consistent with assigned roles and responsibilities, to system users within {{param:ir-02_odp.01}} of their assuming an incident response role or responsibility or acquiring system access. (IR-2a.1)
- The {{org:incident-response-team}} shall provide that training when system changes require it. (IR-2a.2)
- The {{org:incident-response-team}} shall provide that training {{param:ir-02_odp.02}} thereafter. (IR-2a.3)
- The {{org:incident-response-team}} shall review and update incident response training content {{param:ir-02_odp.03}} and following {{param:ir-02_odp.04}}. (IR-2b)
