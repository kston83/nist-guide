- All personnel shall report suspected incidents to the {{org:incident-response-team}} within {{param:ir-06_odp.01}}. (IR-6a)
- The {{org:incident-response-team}} shall report incident information to {{param:ir-06_odp.02}}. (IR-6b)

:::federal
Federal agencies notify the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) of incidents under FISMA (44 U.S.C. §§ 3553 and 3554). As of September 2026, the [CISA Federal Incident Notification Guidelines](https://www.cisa.gov/federal-incident-notification-guidelines) (effective April 1, 2017) set the time frame: within one hour of the incident being identified by the agency's top-level incident response team, security operations center or IT department.

- The {{org:incident-response-team}} shall report incidents to CISA within one hour of identification, as the CISA Federal Incident Notification Guidelines require. (IR-6b)

:::
