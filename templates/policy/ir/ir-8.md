title: Incident response plan
status: draft
stage: core
typical:
  ir-08_odp.01: the Chief Information Security Officer
  ir-08_odp.02: annually
  ir-08_odp.03: 'the incident response team, led by its designated lead'
  ir-08_odp.04: 'the incident response team members, by role'
  ir-08_odp.05: 'the security operations, legal, communications and human resources functions'
  ir-8_prm_5: everyone who received the plan
---

- The {{org:ciso}} shall develop an incident response plan that provides a roadmap for implementing the incident response capability. (IR-8a.1)
- The plan shall describe the structure and organization of the incident response capability. (IR-8a.2)
- The plan shall describe at a high level how the incident response capability fits into the overall organization. (IR-8a.3)
- The plan shall meet the organization's unique requirements related to its mission, size, structure and functions. (IR-8a.4)
- The plan shall define reportable incidents. (IR-8a.5)
- The plan shall provide metrics for measuring the incident response capability. (IR-8a.6)
- The plan shall define the resources and management support needed to maintain and mature the incident response capability. (IR-8a.7)
- The plan shall address the sharing of incident information. (IR-8a.8)
- The plan shall be reviewed and approved by {{param:ir-08_odp.01}} {{param:ir-08_odp.02}}. (IR-8a.9)
- The plan shall explicitly designate responsibility for incident response to {{param:ir-08_odp.03}}. (IR-8a.10)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall distribute copies of the plan to {{param:ir-08_odp.04}} and {{param:ir-08_odp.05}}. (IR-8b)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall update the plan to address system and organizational changes, and problems found during its implementation, execution or testing. (IR-8c)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall communicate changes to the plan to {{param:ir-8_prm_5}}. (IR-8d)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall protect the plan from unauthorized disclosure and modification. (IR-8e)
