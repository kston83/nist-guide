:::guidance
MA-2(2) is in the High baseline. An IT service management or maintenance management system that schedules work orders, and management tools that carry out and report the work, give the timely, accurate and complete records NIST's discussion describes. Records of requested and scheduled work, not only completed work, are what distinguish this enhancement from MA-2.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall schedule maintenance, repair and replacement actions for the system using {{param:ma-02.02_odp.01}}. (MA-2(2)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall conduct maintenance, repair and replacement actions for the system using {{param:ma-02.02_odp.02}}, where the action can be automated. (MA-2(2)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall document maintenance, repair and replacement actions for the system using {{param:ma-02.02_odp.03}}. (MA-2(2)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure those mechanisms produce up-to-date, accurate and complete records of all maintenance, repair and replacement actions requested, scheduled, in process and completed. (MA-2(2)(b))
