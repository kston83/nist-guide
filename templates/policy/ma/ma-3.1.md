:::guidance
Inspection means checking that a tool is the approved one, in the approved version, with no sign of tampering: for hardware, its seals, serial number and condition; for software, a hash or signature check against the vendor's published value. NIST's discussion of this enhancement has an improperly modified tool, or one containing malicious code, handled as an incident.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the maintenance tools that maintenance personnel bring into the facility or download are inspected for improper or unauthorized modifications before each use on the system. (MA-3(1))
- Personnel shall report a tool found to be modified improperly, or to contain malicious code, as a security incident under the incident response plan (IR-6). (MA-3(1))
