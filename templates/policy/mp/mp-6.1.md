:::guidance
MP-6(1) is in the High baseline. NIST's discussion of this enhancement has organizations review and approve media for sanitization to ensure compliance with records retention policies, and lists what to document: who reviewed and approved the action, the types of media, the files stored on them, the method, the date and time, who sanitized them, the verification and who did it, and the disposal action. [NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/88/r2/final) section 4.5 separates verification (checking the technique completed, by inspecting destruction remnants or the tool's completion status) from validation (deciding whether the result is acceptable, or repeating with a different technique or a stronger method). The [media sanitization record](/templates/forms/media-sanitization-record/) holds every field.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review and approve each media sanitization and disposal action before it is performed, confirming that records retention requirements are met. (MP-6(1))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall track each item of media from its removal from service to its final disposition, so that every item removed has a recorded outcome. (MP-6(1))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall document each media sanitization and disposal action in the media sanitization record, including who reviewed and approved it, the type of media, the files or information stored on them, the sanitization method, the date and time, who performed it, the verification and who performed it, and the disposal action taken. (MP-6(1))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall verify that the sanitization was effective before the media are disposed of; a result that is not accepted shall be repeated with a different technique or a stronger method. (MP-6(1))
