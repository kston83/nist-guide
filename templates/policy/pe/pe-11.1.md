:::guidance
PE-11(1) is in the High baseline. NIST's discussion of this enhancement says it can be met by a secondary commercial power supply or another external power supply. A generator with an automatic transfer switch is the usual way; size it and its fuel for the minimally required operational capability the contingency plan defines.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall provide an alternate power supply for the system that is activated {{param:pe-11.01_odp}} and that can maintain minimally required operational capability in the event of an extended loss of the primary power source. (PE-11(1))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall define the minimally required operational capability in the contingency plan, and the {{org:facilities-manager}} shall keep fuel or supply contracts sufficient to sustain it for the outage duration the plan assumes. (PE-11(1))
