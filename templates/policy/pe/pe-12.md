:::guidance
NIST's PE-12 discussion says emergency lighting applies mainly to facilities with concentrations of system resources, such as data centers, server rooms and mainframe rooms; that emergency lighting provisions are described in the organization's contingency plan; and that if emergency lighting fails or cannot be provided, organizations consider alternate processing sites for power-related contingencies. Building and fire codes usually require emergency lighting already; PE-12 asks that it cover the rooms that hold the system and be maintained.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall employ and maintain automatic emergency lighting for the system that activates in the event of a power outage or disruption and that covers emergency exits and evacuation routes within the facility. (PE-12)
- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall test emergency lighting at least annually, and repair failures promptly. (PE-12)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall describe the emergency lighting provisions for the system in the contingency plan (CP-2). (PE-12)
