:::guidance
PE-13(1) is in the Moderate and High baselines. NIST's discussion of this enhancement says organizations can name personnel, roles and emergency responders when people on the notification list need access authorizations or clearances to enter restricted facilities, and that notification mechanisms may need independent energy sources so a fire does not disable them. Check the notification path in the annual fire system test.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall employ fire detection systems that activate automatically and notify {{param:pe-13.01_odp.01}} and {{param:pe-13.01_odp.02}} in the event of a fire. (PE-13(1))
- The notification path shall have power independent of the facility's main supply, and shall be tested with the fire detection system. (PE-13(1))
