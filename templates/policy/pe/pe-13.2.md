:::guidance
PE-13(2) is in the High baseline. NIST's discussion of this enhancement matches PE-13(1)'s: name personnel and responders where access authorizations are needed to enter, and give the notification mechanism an independent energy source. Use the same notification list as PE-13(1).
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall employ fire suppression systems that activate automatically and notify {{param:pe-13.02_odp.01}} and {{param:pe-13.02_odp.02}}. (PE-13(2)(a))
- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall employ an automatic fire suppression capability when the facility is not staffed on a continuous basis. (PE-13(2)(b))
