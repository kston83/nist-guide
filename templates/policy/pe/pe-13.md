:::guidance
NIST's PE-13 discussion says fire detection and suppression applies mainly to facilities with concentrations of system resources, such as data centers, server rooms and mainframe rooms; that systems that may need an independent energy source include sprinkler systems and smoke detectors; and that an independent energy source, such as a microgrid, is one separate, or separable, from the sources powering the rest of the facility. Building and fire codes and the local fire authority set most of the details; the policy makes sure the rooms that hold the system are covered and the systems are maintained. For a system hosted by a provider, PE-13 is inherited.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall employ and maintain fire detection and suppression systems, supported by an independent energy source, that cover each area containing system components. (PE-13)
- Fire detection and suppression systems shall be inspected, tested and maintained as the applicable fire code and the manufacturer require, and the records kept. (PE-13)
- Where the suppression system could itself damage equipment, such as water sprinklers in a server room, the {{org:facilities-manager}} shall record the choice and the reason, with the system owner's agreement. (PE-13)
