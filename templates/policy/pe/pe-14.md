:::guidance
NIST's PE-14 discussion says environmental controls apply mainly to facilities with concentrations of system resources, such as data centers, mainframe rooms and server rooms, and that insufficient controls, especially in harsh environments, can significantly affect the availability of systems that support mission and business functions. Pressure and radiation matter only in special environments; add them, or another control such as dust, where the site needs it. A server room cooled by the building's office air conditioning, which shuts off at night or on weekends, is a common finding. For a system hosted by a provider, PE-14 is inherited.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall maintain {{param:pe-14_odp.01}} levels within the facility where the system resides at {{param:pe-14_odp.03}}. (PE-14a)
- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall monitor environmental control levels {{param:pe-14_odp.04}}. (PE-14b)
- Alerts shall go to staff who can respond at any hour, and an alert that is not resolved within the time the contingency plan allows shall start the contingency plan's notification procedures (CP-2). (PE-14b)
- Cooling for areas that contain system components shall run independently of the building's office schedule, with enough capacity or redundancy that the loss of one unit does not take the room out of range. (PE-14a)
