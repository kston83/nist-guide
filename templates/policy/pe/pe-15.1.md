:::guidance
PE-15(1) is in the High baseline. NIST's discussion of this enhancement lists notification systems, water detection sensors and alarms as automated mechanisms. Alert the same people as the environmental monitoring in PE-14, so one on-call path covers both.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall detect the presence of water near the system and alert {{param:pe-15.01_odp.01}} using {{param:pe-15.01_odp.02}}. (PE-15(1))
- The water detection sensors and their alerts shall be tested at least annually. (PE-15(1))
