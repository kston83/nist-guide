:::guidance
NIST's PE-15 discussion says water damage protection applies mainly to facilities with concentrations of system resources, such as data centers, server rooms and mainframe rooms, and that isolation valves can be used in addition to, or instead of, master shutoff valves to shut off water in specific areas without affecting the whole organization. Assessors ask staff where the valve is and check that it is labeled and reachable. For a system hosted by a provider, PE-15 is inherited.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall protect the system from damage resulting from water leakage by providing master shutoff or isolation valves that are accessible, working properly, and known to key personnel. (PE-15)
- The valves shall be labeled, shown on a facility plan kept with the contingency plan, kept clear of obstructions, and exercised at least annually. (PE-15)
- Key personnel, including the staff who work in areas that contain system components, shall be shown the valve locations and how to operate them. (PE-15)
- Water pipes shall not run above system components where it can be avoided; where they do, the {{org:facilities-manager}} shall record the risk and the protection used, such as drip trays or leak detection. (PE-15)
