:::guidance
NIST's PE-16 discussion says enforcing authorizations for components entering and leaving the facility may require restricting access to delivery areas and isolating them from the system and media libraries. PE-16 is where the other families' removal rules meet the door: a component leaving for repair needs the system owner's approval and sanitized media under the Maintenance Policy (MA-2), media leaving a controlled area travel under the Media Protection Policy (MP-5), and arriving components are checked against the order and recorded in the component inventory (CM-8) before they are connected. Assessors pick a recent delivery and a recent removal and follow each through the records.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall authorize and control {{param:pe-16_odp.01}} entering the facility, receiving them only in a delivery area that is separated from areas that contain system components and media libraries. (PE-16a)
- Arriving components shall be checked against the purchase order or shipping record and for signs of tampering before they are moved out of the delivery area, and reported under the incident response plan if they show any. (PE-16a)
- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall authorize and control {{param:pe-16_odp.02}} exiting the facility, letting a component leave only with the {{org:system-owner}}'s approval and, for a component with storage, a record that its media were sanitized or removed (MA-2, MP-6). (PE-16a)
- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall maintain records of the system components entering and exiting the facility: the item and serial number, the date, who delivered or carried it, who authorized it, and where it went. (PE-16b)
- Laptops and other devices issued to individuals may leave and return with their assigned user without a record of each trip, as long as they are recorded in the component inventory against that user. (PE-16a)
