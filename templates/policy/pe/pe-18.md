:::guidance
PE-18 is in the High baseline. NIST's PE-18 discussion lists physical and environmental hazards, including floods, fires, tornadoes, earthquakes, hurricanes, terrorism, vandalism, an electromagnetic pulse and electrical interference, and asks organizations to consider entry points where unauthorized individuals, though not granted access, might be near systems, which can increase the risk of interception with wireless sniffers or microphones. Server rooms on a basement level below a water source, or beside a public corridor or loading dock, are the usual examples.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} and the {{org:system-owner}} shall position system components within the facility to minimize potential damage from {{param:pe-18_odp}} and to minimize the opportunity for unauthorized access. (PE-18)
- Areas that contain system components shall not adjoin public areas, delivery areas or exterior walls at ground level where an alternative exists, and the reasons for any exception shall be recorded with the risk assessment (RA-3). (PE-18)
