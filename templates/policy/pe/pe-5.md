:::guidance
PE-5 is in the Moderate and High baselines. NIST's PE-5 discussion lists the ways to control output: output devices in locked rooms or secured areas with keypad or card reader access, devices placed where personnel can monitor them, monitor or screen filters, and headphones. Its examples of output devices are monitors, printers, scanners, audio devices, fax machines and copiers. Secure print release, where a job prints only after the user authenticates at the device, is the usual way to meet PE-5 for shared printers. The storage inside multifunction printers and copiers is media, sanitized under the Media Protection Policy before the device leaves (MP-6).
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} and the {{org:facilities-manager}} shall control physical access to output from {{param:pe-05_odp}} to prevent unauthorized individuals from obtaining the output. (PE-5)
- Shared printers and copiers that output information not approved for public release shall release a job only after the user authenticates at the device, or shall be placed in a room limited to authorized individuals. (PE-5)
- Monitors in reception and other areas visitors can see shall be positioned away from them or fitted with privacy filters. (PE-5)
- Users shall collect output promptly; uncollected output shall be removed and destroyed at least daily. (PE-5)
