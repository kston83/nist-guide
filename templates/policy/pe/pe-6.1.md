:::guidance
PE-6(1) is in the Moderate and High baselines. NIST's discussion of this enhancement says intrusion alarms alert security personnel when unauthorized access is attempted, working with barriers, access control systems and guards by triggering a response when they are breached; sensors include motion, contact and glass-break sensors, and surveillance equipment includes cameras at strategic locations. Keep video only as long as the records schedule allows, and treat recordings as personally identifiable information.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall monitor physical access to the facility where the system resides using physical intrusion alarms and surveillance equipment. (PE-6(1))
- Intrusion alarms shall cover the facility's entry points and each area that contains system components when it is unoccupied, and shall alert guards, a monitoring service or designated staff who respond. (PE-6(1))
- Surveillance cameras shall cover the entry points to the facility and to each area that contains system components, and recordings shall be kept for the period the records schedule sets, with access limited to authorized staff. (PE-6(1))
- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall test the alarms and check the cameras at least annually, and repair faults promptly. (PE-6(1))
