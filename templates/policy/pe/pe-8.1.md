:::guidance
PE-8(1) is in the High baseline. NIST's discussion of this enhancement says visitor access records may be kept in a database accessible to organizational personnel, and that automated access makes regular reviews easier. An electronic visitor management system that prints the visitor badge and records the escort meets the maintenance half; the review half needs reports, such as open visits and badges not returned, that someone reads on the PE-8 schedule.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall maintain visitor access records using {{param:pe-08.01_odp.01}}. (PE-8(1))
- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall review visitor access records using {{param:pe-08.01_odp.02}}. (PE-8(1))
