:::guidance
NIST's PE-8 discussion lists what visitor access records include: names and organizations of visitors, visitor signatures, forms of identification, dates of access, entry and departure times, purpose of visits, and names and organizations of the individuals visited. It says reviews determine whether access authorizations are current and still required, and that records are not required for publicly accessible areas. Where the organization adopts the Privacy baseline, PE-8(3) limits these elements to those the privacy risk assessment identifies. The [visitor log](/templates/forms/visitor-log/) holds the fields, the reviews and the anomalies reported. Assessors pick a few dates and ask for that day's records, then for the last review.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall maintain visitor access records to the facility where the system resides for {{param:pe-08_odp.01}}. (PE-8a)
- Each visitor record shall include the visitor's name and organization, the form of identification checked, the date, entry and departure times, the purpose of the visit, the name of the person visited, the badge issued and the escort, subject to the limits PE-8(3) sets where it applies. (PE-8a)
- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall review visitor access records {{param:pe-08_odp.02}}, checking for visitors with no departure time, badges not returned, visits outside normal hours, repeated visits that suggest the person needs a PE-2 authorization instead, and visits without an escort. (PE-8b)
- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall report anomalies in visitor access records to {{param:pe-08_odp.03}}, and the security operations team shall handle any that suggest an incident under the incident response plan (IR-6). (PE-8c)

:::federal
The National Archives and Records Administration's [General Records Schedule 5.6](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/grs/grs05-6.pdf), Security Management Records (GRS Transmittal 35, May 2024), covers visitor processing records, such as registers or logs of visitors, contractors and service personnel. Item 110, for areas requiring the highest level of security awareness, including areas the Interagency Security Committee designates as Facility Security Level V, says destroy when 5 years old; item 111, for all other facility security areas, including Facility Security Levels I to IV, says destroy when 2 years old. Both allow longer retention if required for business use. As of October 2026.

- Visitor access records shall be kept for at least 5 years for areas at Facility Security Level V and at least 2 years for other areas, as GRS 5.6 items 110 and 111 set, unless the agency's own approved records schedule sets a different period. (PE-8a)

:::
