:::guidance
PE-9 is in the Moderate and High baselines. NIST's PE-9 discussion has organizations decide the protection needed for power equipment and cabling inside and outside their facilities, such as internal cabling and uninterruptible power supplies in offices or data centers, and generators and power cabling outside buildings. For a system hosted by a provider, PE-9 is inherited for the provider's facility.
:::

- The {{org:facilities-manager}} shall protect power equipment and power cabling for the system from damage and destruction. (PE-9)
- Power distribution units, uninterruptible power supplies, transfer switches and generators that serve the system shall be in locked rooms or enclosures with access limited to authorized staff, and outdoor equipment shall be fenced or otherwise protected from vehicles and tampering. (PE-9)
- Power cabling shall be routed and labeled so that it is protected from accidental damage, kept separate from data cabling where practical, and not run across walkways. (PE-9)
- Power equipment shall be maintained as the manufacturer specifies, and the maintenance recorded. (PE-9)
