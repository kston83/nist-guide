:::guidance
Tailoring adjusts the baseline to the system: identifying common controls, scoping out controls that do not apply, setting parameter values, adding controls for specific threats, and applying overlays. Assessors look for a documented reason for every control removed or marked not applicable.
:::

- Each {{org:system-owner}} shall tailor the selected control baseline by applying specified tailoring actions. (PL-11)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall document each tailoring decision, with its rationale, in the system security plan, and have the authorizing official approve it with the plan. (PL-11)
