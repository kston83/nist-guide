:::guidance
PL-2 lists fifteen things a system's security and privacy plans must contain. The [System Security Plan template](/templates/plans/system-security-plan/) has a section for each, so the statements below require the plan and point to its contents rather than repeating all fifteen. A system that processes personally identifiable information can keep its privacy plan as part of the security plan or as a separate document; either way, both must meet PL-2.
:::

- Each {{org:system-owner}} shall develop a security plan and a privacy plan for the system, as one document or two, that contain every element PL-2a.1 to a.14 lists. (PL-2a)
- The plans shall identify the security- and privacy-related activities affecting the system that require planning and coordination with {{param:pl-02_odp.01}}. (PL-2a.14)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall have the plans reviewed and approved by the authorizing official, or a designated representative, before the plans are implemented. (PL-2a.15)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall distribute copies of the plans, and communicate later changes to them, to {{param:pl-02_odp.02}}. (PL-2b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review the plans {{param:pl-02_odp.03}}. (PL-2c)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall update the plans to address changes to the system and its environment of operation, and problems found during plan implementation or control assessments. (PL-2d)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall protect the plans from unauthorized disclosure and modification. (PL-2e)
