---
control: pl-4.1
title: Social media and external site and application usage restrictions
status: draft
stage: foundation
---

- The rules of behavior shall restrict the use of social media, social networking sites, and external sites and applications. (PL-4(1)(a))
- The rules of behavior shall restrict the posting of organizational information on public websites. (PL-4(1)(b))
- The rules of behavior shall restrict the use of organization-provided identifiers, such as email addresses, and authentication secrets, such as passwords, to create accounts on external sites and applications. (PL-4(1)(c))
