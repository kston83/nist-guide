:::guidance
The architectures describe how the system is built to protect information and to limit privacy risk: the protection approach, the trust boundaries, and what the system assumes about the services it relies on. Most systems keep them as a section of the system security plan with a diagram, rather than as a separate document.
:::

- Each {{org:system-owner}} shall develop a security architecture and a privacy architecture for the system. (PL-8a)
- The architectures shall describe the requirements and approach for protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of organizational information. (PL-8a.1)
- The architectures shall describe the requirements and approach for processing personally identifiable information so as to minimize privacy risk to individuals. (PL-8a.2)
- The architectures shall describe how they are integrated into and support the enterprise architecture. (PL-8a.3)
- The architectures shall describe any assumptions about, and dependencies on, external systems and services. (PL-8a.4)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review and update the architectures {{param:pl-08_odp}} to reflect changes in the enterprise architecture. (PL-8b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall reflect planned architecture changes in the security and privacy plans, the concept of operations, the criticality analysis, organizational procedures, and procurements and acquisitions. (PL-8c)
