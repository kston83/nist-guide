:::guidance
PM-10 makes authorization an organization-wide process rather than a one-off per system. The program plan should name who may serve as an authorizing official and how their decisions feed the risk management strategy.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall manage the security and privacy state of organizational systems, and the environments they operate in, through an authorization process. (PM-10a)
- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall designate the individuals who fill the roles in the risk management process, including an authorizing official for each system. (PM-10b)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall integrate the authorization process into the organization-wide risk management program. (PM-10c)
