- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall ensure the organization's mission and business processes are defined with consideration for information security, privacy and the resulting risk to organizational operations and assets, individuals, other organizations and the Nation. (PM-11a)
- The {{org:ciso}} and the {{org:privacy-official}} shall determine the information protection and personally identifiable information processing needs that arise from those processes. (PM-11b)
- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall review and revise the mission and business processes {{param:pm-11_odp}}. (PM-11c)
