:::guidance
An insider threat program brings together information that security, human resources, legal and privacy each hold, so that concerning behavior is noticed and handled consistently. Involve the privacy official from the start: the program handles personal information about the workforce.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall implement an insider threat program. (PM-12)
- The program shall include a cross-discipline insider threat incident handling team, with members from at least security, human resources, legal and privacy. (PM-12)
