title: 'Testing, training and monitoring'
status: draft
stage: core

- The {{org:ciso}} shall maintain a process that ensures the organization's plans for security and privacy testing, training and monitoring of organizational systems are developed and maintained. (PM-14a.1)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall track that those plans continue to be carried out. (PM-14a.2)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall review the testing, training and monitoring plans for consistency with the risk management strategy and the organization-wide priorities for risk response. (PM-14b)
