---
control: pm-15
title: Security and privacy groups and associations
status: draft
stage: core
---

- The {{org:ciso}} shall establish and maintain contact with selected security and privacy groups and associations, and record which ones. (PM-15)
- The organization shall use those contacts to support ongoing security and privacy education and training for its personnel. (PM-15a)
- The organization shall use those contacts to stay current with recommended security and privacy practices, techniques and technologies. (PM-15b)
- The organization shall use those contacts to share current security and privacy information, including threats, vulnerabilities and incidents. (PM-15c)
