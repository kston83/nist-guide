:::guidance
Controlled unclassified information (CUI) is information the U.S. government creates or owns that a law, regulation or government-wide policy requires to be protected. PM-17 applies wherever the organization shares CUI with external systems, or holds CUI under a contract or agreement. If it handles no CUI, keep the heading and record that decision in the program plan.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall establish policy and procedures to ensure that requirements for protecting controlled unclassified information processed, stored or transmitted on external systems are implemented in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, policies and standards. (PM-17a)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall review and update that policy {{param:pm-17_odp.01}}. (PM-17b)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall review and update those procedures {{param:pm-17_odp.02}}. (PM-17b)

:::federal
Agencies must require non-federal systems that handle CUI to protect it with NIST SP 800-171, which [32 CFR 2002.14(h)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/subtitle-B/chapter-XX/part-2002/subpart-A/section-2002.14) incorporates by reference. [NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/171/r3/final) (May 2024) is the current revision, as of September 2026.

- Each agreement that shares CUI with a non-federal organization shall require the recipient to protect it as 32 CFR 2002.14(h) requires. (PM-17a)

:::
