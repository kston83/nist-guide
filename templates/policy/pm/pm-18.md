:::guidance
The privacy program plan is the privacy counterpart of the information security program plan (PM-1). Many organizations keep them as two chapters of one program document; assessors accept that if each chapter meets its own control.
:::

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall develop and disseminate an organization-wide privacy program plan that gives an overview of the privacy program. (PM-18a)
- The plan shall describe the structure of the privacy program and the resources dedicated to it. (PM-18a.1)
- The plan shall give an overview of the requirements for the privacy program, and describe the privacy program management controls and common controls in place or planned to meet them. (PM-18a.2)
- The plan shall include the role of the {{org:privacy-official}}, and identify the roles and responsibilities of other privacy officials and staff. (PM-18a.3)
- The plan shall describe management commitment, compliance, and the strategic goals and objectives of the privacy program. (PM-18a.4)
- The plan shall reflect coordination among the organizational entities responsible for the different aspects of privacy. (PM-18a.5)
- The {{org:senior-leader}}, as the senior official accountable for the privacy risk the organization incurs, shall approve the plan. (PM-18a.6)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall update the plan {{param:pm-18_odp}}, and to address changes in privacy law and policy, organizational changes, and problems found during plan implementation or privacy control assessments. (PM-18b)

:::federal
[OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf), Appendix II, requires each agency to develop, maintain and update an organization-wide privacy program plan.

- The plan shall address the changes in federal privacy laws and policy that apply to the agency. (PM-18b)

:::
