:::guidance
PM-2 asks for one named senior official who owns the security program. Assessors look for the appointment in writing (a delegation memo or charter) and for evidence that the official has budget and staff, not just a title.
:::

- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall appoint a {{org:ciso}} to coordinate, develop, implement and maintain the organization-wide information security program. (PM-2)
- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall record the appointment in writing and give the {{org:ciso}} the mission and resources the role needs. (PM-2)

:::federal
FISMA requires the agency head to designate a senior agency information security officer who has information security duties as that official's primary duty ([44 U.S.C. § 3554(a)(3)(A)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3554&num=0&edition=prelim)).

- The {{org:ciso}} shall be the senior agency information security officer the agency head designates under 44 U.S.C. § 3554(a)(3)(A), with information security duties as that official's primary duty. (PM-2)

:::
