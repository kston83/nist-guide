---
control: pm-20.1
title: 'Privacy policies on websites, applications and digital services'
status: draft
stage: core
---

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall ensure a privacy policy is posted on every external-facing website, mobile application and other digital service. (PM-20(1))
- Each privacy policy shall be written in plain language and organized so that it is easy to understand and navigate. (PM-20(1)(a))
- Each privacy policy shall give the public the information it needs to decide whether and how to interact with the organization. (PM-20(1)(b))
- Each privacy policy shall be updated whenever the organization makes a substantive change to the practices it describes, and shall show the date of the latest change. (PM-20(1)(c))
