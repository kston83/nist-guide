---
control: pm-20
title: Dissemination of privacy program information
status: draft
stage: core
---

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall maintain a privacy webpage on the organization's principal public website as the central source of information about the privacy program. (PM-20)
- The page shall give the public information about the organization's privacy activities and a way to contact the {{org:privacy-official}}. (PM-20a)
- The page shall make the organization's privacy practices and reports publicly available. (PM-20b)
- The page shall list a public email address or phone line for feedback and questions to the privacy office. (PM-20c)

:::federal
[OMB M-23-22](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/M-23-22-Delivering-a-Digital-First-Public-Experience.pdf), Delivering a Digital-First Public Experience (September 2023), requires each agency's privacy program page to be located at `www.[agency].gov/privacy`, as of September 2026.

- The privacy webpage shall be located at the address M-23-22 requires, `www.[agency].gov/privacy`. (PM-20)

:::
