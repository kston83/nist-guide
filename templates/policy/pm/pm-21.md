:::guidance
The retention period in PM-21b is fixed by NIST's control text, not a parameter. Keep the accounting where it can be searched by individual, so a request under PM-21c can be answered.
:::

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall ensure an accurate accounting is kept of each disclosure of personally identifiable information, recording the date, nature and purpose of the disclosure. (PM-21a.1)
- The accounting shall record the name and address, or other contact information, of the individual or organization that received the disclosure. (PM-21a.2)
- The accounting shall be kept for as long as the personally identifiable information is maintained or five years after the disclosure, whichever is longer. (PM-21b)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall make the accounting available, on request, to the individual the information is about. (PM-21c)

:::federal
For records in a Privacy Act system of records, [5 U.S.C. § 552a(c)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim) sets this accounting. It excludes disclosures to agency employees who need the record for their duties (subsection (b)(1)) and disclosures the Freedom of Information Act requires (subsection (b)(2)). It does not require the accounting to be shown to the individual for law enforcement disclosures under subsection (b)(7).

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall keep the accounting of disclosures from each system of records as 5 U.S.C. § 552a(c) requires. (PM-21)

:::
