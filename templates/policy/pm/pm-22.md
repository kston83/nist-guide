---
control: pm-22
title: Personally identifiable information quality management
status: draft
stage: core
---

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall develop and document organization-wide policies and procedures for reviewing personally identifiable information for accuracy, relevance, timeliness and completeness across the information life cycle. (PM-22a)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall develop and document organization-wide policies and procedures for correcting or deleting inaccurate or outdated personally identifiable information. (PM-22b)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall develop and document organization-wide policies and procedures for notifying individuals or other appropriate entities of corrected or deleted personally identifiable information. (PM-22c)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall develop and document organization-wide policies and procedures for appeals of adverse decisions on correction or deletion requests. (PM-22d)
