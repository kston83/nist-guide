- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall develop, document and implement policies and procedures for using personally identifiable information in internal testing, training and research. (PM-25a)
- Internal testing, training and research shall use no more personally identifiable information than the purpose needs, and shall use synthetic or de-identified data wherever it serves the purpose. (PM-25b)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall authorize each use of personally identifiable information for internal testing, training or research before it begins. (PM-25c)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall review those policies {{param:pm-25_odp.01}} and update them {{param:pm-25_odp.02}}. (PM-25d)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall review those procedures {{param:pm-25_odp.03}} and update them {{param:pm-25_odp.04}}. (PM-25d)
