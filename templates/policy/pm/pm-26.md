- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall implement a process for receiving and responding to complaints, concerns and questions from individuals about the organization's security and privacy practices. (PM-26)
- The process shall offer ways to file that are easy to use and readily accessible to the public. (PM-26a)
- The process shall tell individuals all the information they need to file a complaint successfully. (PM-26b)
- The process shall track each complaint so that it is reviewed within {{param:pm-26_odp.01}} and addressed within {{param:pm-26_odp.02}}. (PM-26c)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall acknowledge receipt of each complaint, concern or question within {{param:pm-26_odp.03}}. (PM-26d)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall respond to each complaint, concern or question within {{param:pm-26_odp.04}}. (PM-26e)
