typical:
  pm-27_odp.01: 'an annual privacy program report, summaries of privacy impact assessments, and breach reports'
  pm-27_odp.02: 'the board of directors or its audit committee, and regulators where law requires'
  pm-27_odp.03: 'the senior leader, the chief information security officer and general counsel'
  pm-27_odp.04: annually
---

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall develop {{param:pm-27_odp.01}}. (PM-27a)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall disseminate the reports to {{param:pm-27_odp.02}}, to demonstrate accountability with statutory, regulatory and policy privacy mandates. (PM-27a.1)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall disseminate the reports to {{param:pm-27_odp.03}} and other personnel responsible for monitoring privacy program compliance. (PM-27a.2)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall review and update the reports {{param:pm-27_odp.04}}. (PM-27b)
