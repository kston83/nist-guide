:::guidance
Risk framing is the context every risk decision uses: the assumptions, constraints, priorities and risk tolerance. Most organizations record it as a section of the risk management strategy (PM-9).
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall identify and document the assumptions affecting risk assessment, risk response and risk monitoring. (PM-28a.1)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall identify and document the constraints affecting risk assessment, risk response and risk monitoring. (PM-28a.2)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall identify and document the priorities and trade-offs the organization considers in managing risk. (PM-28a.3)
- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall set and document the organization's risk tolerance. (PM-28a.4)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall distribute the results of risk framing to {{param:pm-28_odp.01}}. (PM-28b)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall review and update the risk framing {{param:pm-28_odp.02}}. (PM-28c)
