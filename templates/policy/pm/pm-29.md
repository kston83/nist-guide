title: Risk management program leadership roles
status: draft

- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall appoint a senior accountable official for risk management to align the organization's information security and privacy management processes with its strategic, operational and budgetary planning. (PM-29a)
- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall establish a risk executive function to view and analyze risk from an organization-wide perspective and keep risk management consistent across the organization. (PM-29b)
