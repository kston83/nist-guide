:::guidance
PM-3 ties the programs to the budget. Evidence is the security and privacy line items in each capital planning or investment request, and a record of any request that left them out and why.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} and the {{org:privacy-official}} shall identify the resources the information security and privacy programs need, for each capital planning and investment request. (PM-3a)
- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall include those resources in capital planning and investment requests, and document every exception. (PM-3a)
- The {{org:ciso}} and the {{org:privacy-official}} shall prepare the security and privacy documentation that capital planning and investment requests require under applicable laws, regulations, policies and standards. (PM-3b)
- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall make the planned information security and privacy resources available for expenditure. (PM-3c)
