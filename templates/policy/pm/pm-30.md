:::guidance
The supply chain risk management strategy is organization-wide; the supply chain risk management plan (SR-2) applies it to systems. NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1 describes both ([Update 1, November 2024](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/161/r1/upd1/final), current as of September 2026).
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall develop an organization-wide strategy for managing supply chain risks associated with the development, acquisition, maintenance and disposal of systems, system components and system services. (PM-30a)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall implement the supply chain risk management strategy consistently across the organization. (PM-30b)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall review and update the supply chain risk management strategy {{param:pm-30_odp}}, and whenever organizational changes require it. (PM-30c)
