:::guidance
PM-4 is the organization-level process; CA-5 is each system's plan of action and milestones (POA&M). Use one format across systems, such as the [POA&M template](/templates/forms/plan-of-action-and-milestones/), so the program can roll items up and compare them against the risk management strategy.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall maintain a process that ensures plans of action and milestones are developed and maintained for the information security, privacy and supply chain risk management programs and for each organizational system. (PM-4a.1)
- Each plan of action and milestones shall document the remedial actions planned to respond to risk to organizational operations and assets, individuals, other organizations and the Nation. (PM-4a.2)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall report plans of action and milestones to the {{org:senior-leader}} {{fill:how often, for example quarterly}}, and to regulators and customers as laws and contracts require. (PM-4a.3)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall review plans of action and milestones for consistency with the risk management strategy and the organization-wide priorities for risk response. (PM-4b)
