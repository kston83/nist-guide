:::guidance
Choose a few measures that show whether the programs work, such as the share of systems with a current authorization, overdue plan of action items, and remediation within the required times. Report the same measures each period so trends show.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall develop, monitor and report on measures of performance for the information security program. (PM-6)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall develop, monitor and report on measures of performance for the privacy program. (PM-6)
- The {{org:ciso}} and the {{org:privacy-official}} shall report the results to the {{org:senior-leader}}. (PM-6)
