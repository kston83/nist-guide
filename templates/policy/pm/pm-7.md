- The {{org:ciso}} shall ensure the enterprise architecture is developed and maintained with consideration for information security, privacy and the resulting risk to organizational operations and assets, individuals, other organizations and the Nation. (PM-7)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall review changes to the enterprise architecture that affect the processing of personally identifiable information. (PM-7)
