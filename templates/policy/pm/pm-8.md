:::guidance
PM-8 applies where the organization owns or operates critical infrastructure or key resources, or helps protect them. If it does neither, keep the heading and record that decision in the program plan.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} and the {{org:privacy-official}} shall address information security and privacy issues when the organization develops, documents and updates its critical infrastructure and key resources protection plan. (PM-8)
