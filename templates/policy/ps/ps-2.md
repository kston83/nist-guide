:::guidance
A risk designation rates each position by the harm a person in it could do through misconduct, usually low, moderate or high. Positions with privileged access, control of money or access to large amounts of sensitive information rate higher. The designation sets the screening each position needs (PS-3), so write the screening criteria for each level in one table.
:::

- The {{org:hr-office}} shall assign a risk designation to every organizational position, in consultation with the {{org:ciso}}. (PS-2a)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall establish screening criteria for the individuals who fill each position, according to its risk designation. (PS-2b)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall review and update position risk designations {{param:ps-02_odp}}. (PS-2c)

:::federal
[5 CFR 731.106](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-731.106) (OPM; last amended June 30, 2026) requires the agency head to designate every covered position, including positions filled by contractor employees, "at high, moderate, or low risk level" by the position's potential for adverse impact to the efficiency or integrity of the service. Each such position also gets a sensitivity designation: Special-Sensitive, Critical-Sensitive, Noncritical-Sensitive or Non-sensitive (731.106(c)(2)); [5 CFR 1400.201](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1400.201) defines the levels for national security positions. OPM and the Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency provide the [Position Designation Tool](https://www.opm.gov/suitability/suitability-executive-agent/position-designation-tool/) for making these designations. Checked September 2026.

- The {{org:hr-office}} shall designate each covered position, including positions filled by contractor employees, at a high, moderate or low risk level, as 5 CFR 731.106(a) requires. (PS-2a)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall give each covered position a sensitivity designation under 5 CFR 731.106(c)(2) and 5 CFR part 1400. (PS-2a)

:::
