:::guidance
Screening is what the position's risk designation calls for: identity and employment checks at the low level, and more, such as criminal history checks, at higher levels. Employment and privacy laws limit what may be checked and how, and they vary by country and state, so settle the screening criteria with legal counsel. Assessors compare screening completion dates with account creation dates.
:::

- The {{org:hr-office}} shall screen each individual, according to the screening criteria for their position, before the individual is authorized to access a system. (PS-3a)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall rescreen individuals when {{param:ps-03_odp.01}}, and, where the screening criteria call for periodic rescreening, {{param:ps-03_odp.02}}. (PS-3b)

:::federal
[5 CFR 731.106](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-731.106) (as amended June 30, 2026) requires a background investigation for each individual entering a covered position, initiated before appointment where possible (731.106(c)(1)), and bars criminal or credit history inquiries before a conditional offer of employment, with limited exceptions (731.106(g)). Rescreening is now continuous vetting: "Continuous vetting for an individual in a public trust position satisfies the requirement for a periodic reinvestigation" (731.106(d)(1)), and an upgraded investigation for a move to a higher risk level "should be initiated within 14 days" (731.106(e)). [FIPS 201-3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/201-3/final) (January 2022), section 2.2, requires the FBI National Criminal History Check to be completed and favorably adjudicated before a PIV Card is issued to someone with no prior investigation. Checked September 2026.

- The {{org:hr-office}} shall initiate the background investigation the position's risk level requires before the individual is appointed, or as soon as possible where it was not initiated in time. (PS-3a)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall not ask an applicant about criminal or credit history before a conditional offer of employment, except as 5 CFR 731.106(g) allows. (PS-3a)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall ensure the FBI National Criminal History Check is completed and favorably adjudicated before a PIV Card is issued to an individual with no prior investigation. (PS-3a)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall ensure individuals in positions subject to investigation are continuously vetted under 5 CFR 731.106(d) and the standards OPM issues. (PS-3b)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall notify each covered employee of the continuous vetting requirements. (PS-3b)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall initiate any upgraded investigation within 14 days after an individual's move to a higher position risk level is final. (PS-3b)

:::
