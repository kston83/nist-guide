:::guidance
A transfer is where access accumulates: people keep what they had and gain what the new job needs. PS-5 applies to reassignments that are permanent or long enough to warrant the actions. The notice time should agree with AC-2h.2. The [onboarding, transfer and termination checklist](/templates/forms/onboarding-transfer-and-termination-checklist/) has a transfer section.
:::

- The {{org:supervisor}} shall review and confirm the ongoing operational need for an individual's current logical and physical access authorizations to systems and facilities when the individual is reassigned or transferred to another position within the organization. (PS-5a)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall initiate {{param:ps-05_odp.01}} within {{param:ps-05_odp.02}} following the formal transfer action. (PS-5b)
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall modify the individual's access authorizations to match any change in operational need caused by the reassignment or transfer. (PS-5c)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall notify {{param:ps-05_odp.03}} of the reassignment or transfer within {{param:ps-05_odp.04}}. (PS-5d)
