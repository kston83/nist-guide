:::guidance
Access agreements include nondisclosure agreements, acceptable use agreements, rules of behavior and conflict-of-interest agreements (SP 800-53 PS-6 discussion). The [access agreement](/templates/forms/access-agreement/) template covers nondisclosure and acceptable use, and takes in the [Rules of Behavior](/templates/forms/rules-of-behavior/) by reference; keeping both on the same annual cycle means one signature event a year. Electronic signatures are acceptable unless organizational policy prohibits them. PS-6 is also in the Privacy baseline.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall develop and document access agreements for organizational systems. (PS-6a)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall review and update the access agreements {{param:ps-06_odp.01}}. (PS-6b)
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall verify that each individual who requires access to organizational information and systems has signed the appropriate access agreements before granting access. (PS-6c.1)
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall verify that each individual re-signs the access agreements when they are updated, and {{param:ps-06_odp.02}}, to keep access to organizational systems. (PS-6c.2)
- The {{org:account-manager}} shall suspend the access of an individual who does not re-sign an updated access agreement until the individual signs it. (PS-6c.2)
