:::guidance
External providers include contractors, service bureaus, and organizations providing development, IT, assessment or security services. Put these requirements in the contract: screening to the same criteria as employees in comparable positions, signed access agreements, required training, and the notice of transfers and terminations. Monitor compliance by comparing the provider's staff roster with the accounts and badges issued to its people.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall establish personnel security requirements, including security roles and responsibilities, for external providers. (PS-7a)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall require external providers to comply with the organization's personnel security policies and procedures. (PS-7b)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall document personnel security requirements for external providers in their contracts or agreements. (PS-7c)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall require external providers to notify {{param:ps-07_odp.01}} within {{param:ps-07_odp.02}} of any transfer or termination of their personnel who hold organizational credentials or badges, or who have system privileges. (PS-7d)
- The {{org:ciso}} shall monitor external provider compliance with personnel security requirements. (PS-7e)
