:::guidance
Sanctions are usually part of the general human resources disciplinary process; this policy makes security and privacy violations subject to it and names who is told. Describe the sanctions in the access agreement and the rules of behavior so people know them before they sign. NIST advises consulting legal counsel on sanctions matters.
:::

- The {{org:hr-office}} shall employ a formal sanctions process for individuals who fail to comply with the organization's information security and privacy policies and procedures. (PS-8a)
- The {{org:hr-office}} shall notify {{param:ps-08_odp.01}} within {{param:ps-08_odp.02}} when a formal employee sanctions process is initiated, identifying the individual sanctioned and the reason for the sanction. (PS-8b)
