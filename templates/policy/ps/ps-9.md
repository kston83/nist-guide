:::guidance
Every position description should carry the general duties all users have, such as following the rules of behavior and reporting incidents. Positions with security or privacy roles, such as system administrators, system security officers and privacy officers, need those roles written in, which also tells the training plan who needs role-based training (AT-3).
:::

- The {{org:hr-office}} shall incorporate security and privacy roles and responsibilities into organizational position descriptions. (PS-9)
