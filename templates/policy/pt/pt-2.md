:::guidance
Processing covers the whole life cycle of personally identifiable information, from creation and collection through use, sharing and storage to disposal. Record the authority for each processing in the system's privacy impact assessment (RA-8), where the privacy office and legal counsel can check it. An organization not governed by statute can rely on its own policies, contracts and the consent of individuals, but it still needs to write the authority down. PT-2 is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall determine and document {{param:pt-02_odp.01}} that permits {{param:pt-02_odp.02}} of personally identifiable information by the system, and record it in the system's privacy impact assessment. (PT-2a)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall confirm the authority with legal counsel before any new processing of personally identifiable information begins. (PT-2a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall restrict {{param:pt-02_odp.03}} of personally identifiable information to only that which is authorized. (PT-2b)

:::federal
The Privacy Act of 1974 requires each agency to "maintain in its records only such information about an individual as is relevant and necessary to accomplish a purpose of the agency required to be accomplished by statute or by executive order of the President" ([5 U.S.C. § 552a(e)(1)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim)), and forbids disclosing a record in a system of records without the individual's written request or prior written consent, except under the conditions in [subsection (b)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim). [OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf) (July 28, 2016), Appendix II, section 5.b, requires agencies to limit the creation, collection, use, processing, storage, maintenance, dissemination and disclosure of personally identifiable information to that which is legally authorized, relevant, and reasonably deemed necessary for the proper performance of agency functions. As of September 2026.

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall record, for each system, the statute or executive order that authorizes the processing. (PT-2a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure records in a system of records are disclosed only with the individual's written request or prior written consent, or under a condition of 5 U.S.C. § 552a(b). (PT-2b)

:::
