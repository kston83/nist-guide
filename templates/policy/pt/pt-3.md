:::guidance
A purpose written down before collection is what lets the organization, and the individual, tell a compatible new use from an incompatible one. The same purposes appear in the privacy impact assessment, the privacy notice (PT-5) and, for federal systems of records, the system of records notice (PT-6). The change control review in CM-3(4) and the impact analysis in CM-4 are the usual monitoring points. PT-3 is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall identify {{param:pt-03_odp.01}} for processing personally identifiable information, and document them in the system's privacy impact assessment. (PT-3a)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall ensure the purposes are described in the organization's public privacy notices and policies. (PT-3b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall restrict {{param:pt-03_odp.02}} of personally identifiable information to only that which is compatible with the identified purposes. (PT-3c)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall monitor changes in the processing of personally identifiable information. (PT-3d)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall implement {{param:pt-03_odp.03}} to ensure any change in processing is made in accordance with {{param:pt-03_odp.04}}. (PT-3d)

:::federal
Under the Privacy Act, a routine use is "the use of such record for a purpose which is compatible with the purpose for which it was collected" ([5 U.S.C. § 552a(a)(7)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim)), and each system of records notice lists the routine uses and their purposes ([subsection (e)(4)(D)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim)). A new or changed routine use needs Federal Register notice and a chance for public comment at least 30 days before publication under paragraph (4)(D) ([subsection (e)(11)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim)). As of September 2026.

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall ensure a new or changed purpose for records in a system of records is published as a routine use, with the notice and comment period 5 U.S.C. § 552a(e)(11) requires, before records are disclosed for it. (PT-3d)

:::
