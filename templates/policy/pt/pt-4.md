:::guidance
Consent works when individuals can understand what they agree to and have a real choice. Where processing is required by law or is needed to deliver what the individual asked for, consent is often the wrong basis, and other controls such as purpose limits (PT-3) do more. Laws in some jurisdictions require consent, or set how it is obtained and withdrawn, for particular data or people; settle those rules with legal counsel. PT-4 is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall ensure {{param:pt-04_odp}} are implemented for individuals to consent to the processing of their personally identifiable information before it is collected. (PT-4)
- Each request for consent shall use plain language and explain what will be processed, for what purpose and with whom it will be shared, so that individuals can make an informed decision. (PT-4)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall keep a record of each consent given and withdrawn. (PT-4)
