:::guidance
Most organizations publish one privacy notice on their privacy page (PM-20) and add short notices where information is collected, such as on a form or a sign-up screen, that link to it. Keep the notice consistent with the purposes and authorities in the privacy impact assessment (PT-2, PT-3). The [privacy notice template](/templates/forms/privacy-notice/) has a full notice, a short notice for points of collection, and a register of where each is posted. PT-5 is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall ensure individuals are given notice about the processing of their personally identifiable information. (PT-5)
- The notice shall be available to individuals when they first interact with the organization, and again at {{param:pt-05_odp.01}}. (PT-5a)
- The notice shall be clear and easy to understand, expressing information about the processing of personally identifiable information in plain language. (PT-5b)
- The notice shall identify the authority that authorizes the processing of personally identifiable information. (PT-5c)
- The notice shall identify the purposes for which personally identifiable information is to be processed. (PT-5d)
- The notice shall include {{param:pt-05_odp.02}}. (PT-5e)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall approve each privacy notice, and each change to one, before it is published. (PT-5)

:::federal
[OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf) (July 28, 2016), Appendix II, section 5.a, requires agencies to maintain and post privacy policies on all agency websites, mobile applications and other digital services, in accordance with the E-Government Act and OMB policy. [Section 208(c) of the E-Government Act of 2002](https://www.congress.gov/107/plaws/publ347/PLAW-107publ347.htm) (Pub. L. 107-347) directs OMB to issue guidance under which a privacy notice on an agency website addresses what information is collected, why, the intended use, with whom it will be shared, what notice or opportunities for consent individuals have, how it will be secured, and the rights of the individual under the Privacy Act and other privacy laws. As of September 2026.

- Each website privacy notice shall address the elements section 208(c)(1)(B) of the E-Government Act lists. (PT-5e)
