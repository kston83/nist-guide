:::guidance
A routine use is a disclosure outside the agency that the system of records notice announces in advance. Review each one against the purpose the records were collected for, and retire uses that are no longer needed. PT-6(1) is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall review all routine uses published in each system of records notice {{param:pt-06.01_odp}} to ensure they remain accurate and compatible with the purpose for which the information was collected. (PT-6(1))

:::federal
[OMB Circular A-108](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A108/omb_circular_a-108.pdf), section 12, requires the privacy controls for a system of records to ensure that all routine uses remain appropriate and that the recipient's use stays compatible with the purpose of collection (12.c). It also records that the agency-wide privacy continuous monitoring program replaced the earlier OMB requirement for annual Privacy Act reviews, so the review frequency is the one the agency's privacy continuous monitoring strategy assigns. As of September 2026.

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall review routine uses at the frequency the agency's privacy continuous monitoring strategy sets for this control. (PT-6(1))

:::
