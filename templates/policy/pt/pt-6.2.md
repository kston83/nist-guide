:::guidance
The Privacy Act lets an agency exempt some systems of records, such as certain law enforcement records, from parts of the Act, but only by publishing a rule. A system that claims no exemptions meets this control by recording that. PT-6(2) is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall review all Privacy Act exemptions claimed for each system of records {{param:pt-06.02_odp}} to ensure they remain appropriate and necessary in accordance with law, have been promulgated as regulations, and are accurately described in the system of records notice. (PT-6(2))

:::federal
The Privacy Act allows exemptions only by rules issued under the rulemaking procedures of 5 U.S.C. § 553, stating the reasons for each ([5 U.S.C. § 552a(j) and (k)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim)). [OMB Circular A-108](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A108/omb_circular_a-108.pdf), section 11, requires each exemption rule to name the system, the provisions exempted and the reasons, and to explain why the exemption is necessary and appropriate, and requires the exemption to be identified in the system of records notice; section 12.d requires the privacy controls to ensure each claimed exemption remains appropriate and necessary. As of September 2026.

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall ensure each exemption claimed for a system of records is issued as a rule under 5 U.S.C. § 553 and identified in the system of records notice, as OMB Circular A-108, section 11, requires. (PT-6(2))

:::
