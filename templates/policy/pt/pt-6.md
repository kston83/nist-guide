:::guidance
A system of records notice tells the public, through the Federal Register, that a U.S. federal agency keeps a group of records about individuals retrieved by name or another identifier, and what it does with them. PT-6 applies to systems that maintain information in a Privacy Act system of records: federal agency systems, and contractor systems operated for an agency. An organization with no such system can record that this control does not apply. PT-6 is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- For each system that processes information to be maintained in a Privacy Act system of records, the {{org:privacy-official}} shall draft the system of records notice in accordance with OMB guidance. (PT-6a)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall submit each new or significantly modified system of records notice to OMB and the appropriate congressional committees for advance review. (PT-6a)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall publish each system of records notice in the Federal Register. (PT-6b)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall keep each system of records notice accurate, up to date and scoped in accordance with policy. (PT-6c)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall tell the {{org:privacy-official}} of any change to the system that could affect its system of records notice before the change is made. (PT-6c)

:::federal
The Privacy Act requires an agency to publish a notice in the Federal Register when it establishes or revises a system of records ([5 U.S.C. § 552a(e)(4)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim)), to give at least 30 days' notice, with a chance for comment, of any new use of the information ([subsection (e)(11)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim)), and to give OMB and the congressional committees advance notice of a proposal to establish or significantly change a system of records ([subsection (r)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim)). [OMB Circular A-108](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A108/omb_circular_a-108.pdf) (reissued December 23, 2016) sets how: a notice drafted with the Office of the Federal Register templates in its Appendices II and III (section 6.c); a report to OMB and Congress at least 30 days before the notice is submitted for publication, with OMB's 30-day review not running at the same time as the Federal Register publication period (section 7.b); a notice of rescindment when the agency stops maintaining the system (section 6.f); and a list of, and links to, every notice on the agency's privacy page (section 15.a). [OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf), Appendix II, section 5.a, requires agencies to ensure notices are published, revised and rescinded as required. As of September 2026.

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall draft each system of records notice with the Office of the Federal Register template in OMB Circular A-108. (PT-6a)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall report each proposal to establish or significantly modify a system of records to OMB and Congress at least 30 days before submitting the notice to the Federal Register. (PT-6a)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall not use a new or significantly modified routine use as the basis for a disclosure until at least 30 days after its Federal Register publication. (PT-6b)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall publish a notice of rescindment when the agency stops maintaining a system of records. (PT-6c)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall list and link every current system of records notice on the agency's privacy program page. (PT-6c)

:::
