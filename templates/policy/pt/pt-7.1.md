:::guidance
Section 7 of the Privacy Act of 1974 (Pub. L. 93-579, printed as a note to 5 U.S.C. § 552a) covers any federal, state or local government agency that asks for a Social Security number, not only federal agencies. It bars denying a right, benefit or privilege for refusing to disclose one, except where a federal statute requires disclosure or for certain systems in place before January 1, 1975, and requires the agency to say whether disclosure is mandatory or voluntary, under what authority, and how the number will be used. Other organizations adopt these statements as good practice or as other laws require. PT-7(1) is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- When a system processes Social Security numbers, the {{org:system-owner}} shall eliminate their unnecessary collection, maintenance and use, and explore alternatives to their use as a personal identifier. (PT-7(1)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure no individual is denied any right, benefit or privilege provided by law because the individual refuses to disclose their Social Security number. (PT-7(1)(b))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall inform each individual asked to disclose their Social Security number whether disclosure is mandatory or voluntary, by what statutory or other authority the number is requested, and what uses will be made of it. (PT-7(1)(c))

:::federal
[OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf) (July 28, 2016), Appendix II, section 5.b, requires agencies to take steps to eliminate unnecessary collection, maintenance and use of Social Security numbers, and to explore alternatives to their use as a personal identifier. Section 7 of the [Privacy Act of 1974](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim) (in the notes to 5 U.S.C. § 552a) sets the rules in PT-7(1)(b) and (c) for federal, state and local government agencies. As of September 2026.

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall review the agency's collection and use of Social Security numbers and record the steps taken to eliminate unnecessary uses, as OMB Circular A-130, Appendix II, requires. (PT-7(1)(a))

:::
