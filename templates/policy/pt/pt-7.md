:::guidance
Some personally identifiable information carries more risk if it is misused, and laws often set extra rules for it: identification numbers, health, financial and biometric information, and information about children are common examples. The privacy risk assessment (RA-3, RA-8) is where a system shows which categories it holds. PT-7 is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall designate the categories of personally identifiable information that need specific processing conditions, such as Social Security numbers, health, financial and biometric information, and information about children. (PT-7)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall apply {{param:pt-07_odp}} to the designated categories of personally identifiable information. (PT-7)
