:::guidance
The supply chain risk assessment looks at the suppliers, products and services a system depends on: where they come from, how they are built and delivered, and what happens if one fails or is compromised. It applies the organization's supply chain risk management strategy (PM-30) and plan (SR-2) to the system.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall assess the supply chain risks associated with {{param:ra-03.01_odp.01}}. (RA-3(1)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall update the supply chain risk assessment {{param:ra-03.01_odp.02}}, when there are significant changes to the relevant supply chain, and when changes to the system, its environment of operation or other conditions call for it. (RA-3(1)(b))
