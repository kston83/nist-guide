:::guidance
A public reporting channel lets researchers and others tell the organization about vulnerabilities they find. It is usually a published vulnerability disclosure policy that says which systems are in scope, how to report, what the organization will do with a report, and that good-faith research is authorized.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall establish a public reporting channel for receiving reports of vulnerabilities in organizational systems and system components. (RA-5(11))
- The {{org:ciso}} shall publish how to use the reporting channel and which systems it covers. (RA-5(11))
- The {{org:security-operations}} shall acknowledge, analyze and track each report received through the channel. (RA-5(11))

:::federal
[CISA BOD 20-01](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/directives/bod-20-01-develop-and-publish-vulnerability-disclosure-policy), Develop and Publish a Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (September 2, 2020), requires federal civilian agencies to publish a vulnerability disclosure policy (in effect as of September 2026).

- The {{org:ciso}} shall publish and maintain a vulnerability disclosure policy that meets CISA BOD 20-01. (RA-5(11))

:::
