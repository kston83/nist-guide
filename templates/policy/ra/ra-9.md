:::guidance
A criticality analysis finds the components and functions whose failure or compromise would stop the system's mission or business functions. The results focus supply chain protections (RA-3(1), SR family) and engineering effort on what matters most.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall identify critical system components and functions by performing a criticality analysis for {{param:ra-09_odp.01}} at {{param:ra-09_odp.02}}. (RA-9)
