:::guidance
SA-10 applies configuration management to the developer, whether an outside supplier or an in-house team. NIST's SA-10 discussion treats the quality of the developer's configuration management as direct evidence that the controls work, and lists the configuration items to protect: design specifications, source code and hardware schematics, the running object code, comparison tools, and test fixtures and documentation. NIST's Secure Software Development Framework, SP 800-218 ([February 2022](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final), version 1.1; version 1.2 is a December 2025 draft; as of September 2026), cites SA-10 for practice PS.1, protecting all forms of code from unauthorized access and tampering.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer of the system, system component or system service to perform configuration management during {{param:sa-10_odp.01}}. (SA-10a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer to document, manage and control the integrity of changes to {{param:sa-10_odp.02}}. (SA-10b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer to implement only organization-approved changes to the system, component or service. (SA-10c)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer to document approved changes and their potential security and privacy impacts. (SA-10d)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer to track security flaws and flaw resolution within the system, component or service, and report findings to {{param:sa-10_odp.03}}. (SA-10e)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure developer changes to an operational system go through the system's configuration change control (CM-3). (SA-10c)
