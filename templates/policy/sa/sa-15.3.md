:::guidance
The developer's criticality analysis feeds the organization's own (RA-9), which often cannot see inside commercial products. NIST's SA-15(3) discussion says developer input is especially important for supply chain criticality analyses and for systems designated as high value assets. The decision points here match those in the RA-9 clause, so both analyses are updated together.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer of the system, system component or system service to perform a criticality analysis at {{param:sa-15.03_odp.01}}. (SA-15(3)(a))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer's criticality analysis to cover the following breadth and depth: {{param:sa-15.3_prm_2}}. (SA-15(3)(b))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use the developer's results in the organization's criticality analysis (RA-9). (SA-15(3))
