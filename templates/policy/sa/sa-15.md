:::guidance
SA-15 asks for a development process that is written down, addresses security and privacy, and keeps its own tools under control, since a compromised build tool compromises everything it builds. NIST's SA-15 discussion says reviews of the process can use maturity models, and that controlling changes to tools supports supply chain risk management. NIST's Secure Software Development Framework, SP 800-218 ([February 2022](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final), version 1.1; version 1.2 is a December 2025 draft; as of September 2026), cites SA-15 for its Prepare the Organization practices, including PO.1, defining security requirements for software development, and PO.3, implementing supporting toolchains. The organization's own secure development policy and standard, based on the SSDF, are Phase 4 templates.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer of the system, system component or system service to follow a documented development process that explicitly addresses security and privacy requirements. (SA-15a.1)
- The documented process shall identify the standards and tools used in the development process. (SA-15a.2)
- The documented process shall document the specific tool options and tool configurations used in the development process. (SA-15a.3)
- The documented process shall document, manage and ensure the integrity of changes to the process and the tools used in development. (SA-15a.4)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall review the development process, standards, tools, tool options and tool configurations {{param:sa-15_odp.01}} to determine whether they can satisfy {{param:sa-15_prm_2}}. (SA-15b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall record each review's findings, and track any shortfall to correction in the plan of action and milestones. (SA-15b)

:::federal
[OMB M-26-05](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/M-26-05-Adopting-a-Risk-based-Approach-to-Software-and-Hardware-Security.pdf), Adopting a Risk-based Approach to Software and Hardware Security (January 23, 2026), rescinds OMB M-22-18 and M-23-16. It states that each agency should validate provider security using secure development principles and a comprehensive risk assessment, requires agencies to develop software and hardware assurance policies and processes that match their risk determinations and mission needs, and names NIST SP 800-218 as a reference. As of September 2026.

- The {{org:ciso}} shall base the security requirements used under SA-15b on the agency's software and hardware assurance policy under OMB M-26-05, and on a risk assessment of the system. (SA-15b)

:::
