  sa-16_odp: 'role-based training for the system''s administrators and security staff on configuring, operating and monitoring the security and privacy functions the developer provides, before the system goes into operation and after each major release, with materials the organization may reuse for in-house training'
---

:::guidance
Controls work only if the people who operate them know how. NIST's SA-16 discussion applies this to both external and in-house developers, lists web-based, classroom and hands-on training, and notes that the organization can instead ask for training materials to run its own. Record completions in the training records (AT-4) with the rest of role-based training.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer of the system, system component or system service to provide {{param:sa-16_odp}} on the correct use and operation of the implemented security and privacy functions, controls and mechanisms. (SA-16)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the personnel who operate those functions complete the training before they are given the related privileges. (SA-16)
