:::guidance
SA-17 is the developer's side of PL-8: NIST's SA-17 discussion directs it at external developers, while PL-8 has the organization build its own security and privacy architecture into the enterprise architecture. The distinction matters most when development is outsourced and the organization must show the delivered design is consistent with its architecture. NIST's discussion points to NIST SP 800-160 Vol. 1 Rev. 1 and ISO/IEC 15408 for security architecture and design.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer of the system, system component or system service to produce a design specification and security and privacy architecture that is consistent with the organization's security and privacy architecture, as an integral part of the organization's enterprise architecture (PL-8). (SA-17a)
- The design specification and architecture shall accurately and completely describe the required security and privacy functionality, and the allocation of controls among physical and logical components. (SA-17b)
- The design specification and architecture shall express how the individual security and privacy functions, mechanisms and services work together to provide the required security and privacy capabilities and a unified approach to protection. (SA-17c)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall have the design specification and architecture reviewed by the security architect and, for a system that processes personally identifiable information, the {{org:privacy-official}}, before the design is accepted. (SA-17)
