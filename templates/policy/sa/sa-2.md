:::guidance
SA-2 makes security and privacy part of the business case for each system, not a cost found later. NIST's SA-2 discussion includes funding for acquisition, sustainment and supply chain risk across the whole life cycle. Evidence is the requirements in the planning documents and a separate security and privacy line in the budget request; the organization-wide side of this is PM-3.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall determine the high-level information security and privacy requirements for the system or system service during mission and business process planning, before an acquisition or development begins. (SA-2a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall determine and document the resources required to protect the system or system service, including acquisition, sustainment and supply chain risk management costs across the system development life cycle, as part of the organization's capital planning and investment control process. (SA-2b)
- The {{org:senior-leader}} shall allocate the documented resources through the capital planning and investment control process, and record the reason for any shortfall. (SA-2b)
- The {{org:ciso}} and the {{org:privacy-official}} shall ensure a discrete line item for information security and a discrete line item for privacy are established in the organization's programming and budgeting documentation for the system. (SA-2c)

:::federal
[OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf), Appendix I, section 4.b(1) to (3), requires agencies to identify and plan for the resources needed to implement information security and privacy programs; to ensure that security and privacy are addressed throughout the life cycle of each agency information system, with security and privacy activities and costs identified and included in IT investment capital plans and budgetary requests; and to plan and budget to upgrade, replace or retire any system for which protections commensurate with risk cannot be effectively implemented. Section 4.e(6) requires the Senior Agency Official for Privacy to review IT capital investment plans and budgetary requests so that privacy requirements, and their costs, are explicitly identified and included. As of September 2026.

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall identify the system's security and privacy activities and costs in its IT investment capital plan and budgetary requests, as OMB Circular A-130, Appendix I, section 4.b(2), requires. (SA-2b)
- For a system that processes personally identifiable information, the {{org:privacy-official}} shall review the system's IT capital investment plan and budgetary requests to confirm that privacy requirements and their costs are explicitly identified and included, as OMB Circular A-130, Appendix I, section 4.e(6), requires. (SA-2c)

:::
