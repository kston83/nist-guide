:::guidance
Developer screening is directed at external developers; in-house developers are screened under PS-3, as NIST's SA-21 discussion notes. The discussion lists clearances, background checks, citizenship and nationality as criteria, and says trustworthiness can also cover the company's ownership and relationships. It adds that the developer provides a list of all individuals authorized to do development work, so the organization can check each has been screened. Outside the federal government, "official government duties" reads as the duties the organization assigns.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require that the developer of {{param:sa-21_odp.01}} has appropriate access authorizations, as determined by assigned {{param:sa-21_odp.02}}. (SA-21a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require that the developer satisfies the following additional personnel screening criteria: {{param:sa-21_odp.03}}. (SA-21b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall put these requirements in the development contract, and check the developer's list of authorized individuals against the screening records before granting access. (SA-21)
