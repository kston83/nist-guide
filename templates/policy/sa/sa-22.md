:::guidance
An unsupported component gets no security updates, so each new vulnerability in it stays open. NIST's SA-22 discussion allows exceptions only where no newer technology exists for a critical capability or the system is too isolated to replace, and suggests isolation, such as no connection to public or uncontrolled networks, to reduce the risk. Track each component's end-of-support date in the component inventory (CM-8), and treat an unsupported component as a flaw under the [patch and flaw remediation standard](/templates/standards/patch-and-flaw-remediation-standard/) until it is replaced.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall replace system components when support for them is no longer available from the developer, vendor or manufacturer. (SA-22a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall plan and budget each replacement before the component's announced end-of-support date. (SA-22a)
- Where a component cannot be replaced by its end-of-support date, the {{org:system-owner}} shall provide the following options for alternative sources of continued support: {{param:sa-22_odp.01}}. (SA-22b)
- The authorizing official shall approve each continued use of an unsupported component, for a set period, with the compensating measures recorded and the replacement tracked in the plan of action and milestones. (SA-22b)

:::federal
[OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf), Appendix I, section 4.i(8), requires agencies to prohibit the use of unsupported information systems and system components, and to give systems and components that cannot be appropriately protected a high priority for upgrade or replacement; section 5.p allows the same exceptions as NIST's SA-22 discussion, with in-house or contracted support for them. CISA [Binding Operational Directive 26-02](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/directives/bod-26-02-mitigating-risk-end-support-edge-devices), Mitigating Risk From End-of-Support Edge Devices (February 5, 2026), covers edge devices: devices on the boundary of an agency's network that are reachable from the public internet. It requires agencies to update supported devices running end-of-support software immediately; to inventory devices on CISA's end-of-support edge device list within 3 months; to decommission listed devices and report to CISA within 12 months; to decommission every end-of-support edge device within 18 months; and, within 24 months, to keep a continuous discovery process and inventory and decommission each edge device by its end-of-support date. As of September 2026.

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall not use an unsupported system component except under an exception that OMB Circular A-130, Appendix I, section 5.p, allows, approved by the authorizing official. (SA-22a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall inventory the system's edge devices, replace or decommission each one that is end of support, and report each decommission to CISA, by the deadlines BOD 26-02 sets. (SA-22a)
- From the directive's 24-month deadline, the {{org:system-owner}} shall decommission each edge device on or before its end-of-support date, as BOD 26-02 requires. (SA-22a)

:::
