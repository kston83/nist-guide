:::guidance
The system development life cycle (SDLC) is where the Risk Management Framework's tasks happen: NIST SP 800-37 Rev. 2 ([December 2018](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/37/r2/final), current as of September 2026) ties each RMF task to an SDLC phase, using the phase names in the typical value. NIST's SA-3 discussion calls for qualified security and privacy staff in SDLC processes and role-based training for them (AT-3). SA-3 leaves the life cycle model to the organization, iterative or sequential; what an assessor checks is that each phase has defined security and privacy activities and someone accountable for them.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall acquire, develop and manage the system using {{param:sa-03_odp}} that incorporates information security and privacy considerations. (SA-3a)
- The {{org:ciso}} and the {{org:privacy-official}} shall define and document the information security and privacy roles and responsibilities for each phase of the system development life cycle. (SA-3b)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall identify, in the system security plan, the individuals who hold information security and privacy roles and responsibilities for the system. (SA-3c)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall integrate the organization's information security and privacy risk management process into the system development life cycle, so that categorization, control selection, implementation, assessment, authorization and monitoring take place in the phases the risk management process assigns them to. (SA-3d)

:::federal
[OMB Circular A-130](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/legacy_drupal_files/omb/circulars/A130/a130revised.pdf), Appendix I, section 4.b(2), requires agencies to ensure that information security and privacy are addressed throughout the life cycle of each agency information system. As of September 2026.

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure information security and privacy are addressed in every phase of the system's life cycle, from planning and acquisition through operation, maintenance and disposal, as OMB Circular A-130, Appendix I, section 4.b(2), requires. (SA-3a)

:::
