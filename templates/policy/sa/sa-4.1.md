title: 'Functional properties of controls'

:::guidance
Functional properties are what a control does as seen at its interfaces: the security or privacy functions a user, administrator or other component can call on, not how the control works inside. NIST's SA-4(1) discussion excludes internal functions and data structures. The description lets the system owner check that each control the contract requires is actually delivered, and gives assessors something to test against.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall require the developer of the system, system component or system service, in the acquisition contract, to provide a description of the functional properties of the controls to be implemented. (SA-4(1))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall accept the description only when it covers every control the contract allocates to the developer. (SA-4(1))
