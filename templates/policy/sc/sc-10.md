title: 'Network disconnect'
status: draft
stage: core
typical:
  sc-10_odp: '30 minutes'
---

:::guidance
SC-10 ends the network connection; AC-12 ends the user's session. Keep the two times consistent: the typical value here matches the 30 minutes AC-12 sets for remote and web sessions.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system terminates the network connection associated with a communications session at the end of the session or after {{param:sc-10_odp}} of inactivity. (SC-10)
