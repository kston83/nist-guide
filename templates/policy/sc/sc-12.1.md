:::guidance
SC-12(1) is in the High baseline. Key escrow or recovery keys held by the organization, such as the recovery keys for full-disk encryption, keep information available when a user loses a key.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall maintain the availability of information when users lose cryptographic keys. (SC-12(1))
