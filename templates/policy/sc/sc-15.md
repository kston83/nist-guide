:::guidance
Collaborative computing devices include cameras, microphones, networked whiteboards and conferencing applications. The point is that nobody can switch on a camera or microphone remotely without the people in the room knowing.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall prohibit remote activation of collaborative computing devices and applications, with these exceptions: {{param:sc-15_odp}}. (SC-15a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure collaborative computing devices and applications give an explicit indication of use to users physically present at the devices. (SC-15b)
