:::guidance
Most organizations obtain certificates for public services from a public certificate authority, which counts as an approved service provider, and run an internal certificate authority under their own certificate policy for internal services. Trust stores should hold only the trust anchors the organization approves.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall ensure public key certificates are issued under {{param:sc-17_odp}}, or obtained from an approved service provider. (SC-17a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall include only approved trust anchors in trust stores or certificate stores the organization manages. (SC-17b)
