---
control: sc-2
title: 'Separation of system and user functionality'
status: draft
stage: core
---

:::guidance
Keep administration interfaces apart from the interfaces users see: separate administrative consoles, ports or network segments, separate administrator accounts, and management planes not reachable from user networks or the internet.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system separates user functionality, including user interface services, from system management functionality. (SC-2)
