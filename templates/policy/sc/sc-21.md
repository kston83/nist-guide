title: 'Secure name and address resolution service (recursive or caching resolver)'
status: draft

:::guidance
In practice this is DNSSEC validation turned on at the resolvers the system uses, or a DNS service that validates.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system requests and performs data origin authentication and data integrity verification on the name and address resolution responses it receives from authoritative sources. (SC-21)
