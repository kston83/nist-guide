:::guidance
Fault tolerance means at least two name servers in different network locations. Role separation means internal and external name servers are separate, so internal names are not published to the internet.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the systems that together provide name and address resolution service for the organization are fault-tolerant and implement internal and external role separation. (SC-22)
