:::guidance
Encryption at rest protects against lost devices, stolen media and direct access to storage; it does not protect against someone who can sign in to the application. Keep keys apart from the data they protect.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall implement cryptographic mechanisms to prevent unauthorized disclosure and modification of {{param:sc-28.01_odp.01}} at rest on {{param:sc-28.01_odp.02}}. (SC-28(1))
