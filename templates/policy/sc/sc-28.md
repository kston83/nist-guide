:::guidance
Protect stored information on servers, in databases and storage services, in backups and on end-user devices. At Moderate and High, SC-28(1) makes the protection cryptographic.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system protects the {{param:sc-28_odp.01}} of {{param:sc-28_odp.02}}. (SC-28)
