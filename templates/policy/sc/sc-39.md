:::guidance
Modern operating systems, hypervisors and container platforms provide separate execution domains; this control is usually inherited. Confirm it for any component that does not, such as some embedded or legacy systems.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system maintains a separate execution domain for each executing system process. (SC-39)
