:::guidance
SC-7(18) is in the High baseline. A boundary device that fails should block traffic, not pass it; confirm the setting for each firewall, gateway and proxy, and test it.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall prevent the system from entering an unsecure state when a boundary protection device fails. (SC-7(18))
