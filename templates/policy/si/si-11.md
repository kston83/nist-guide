:::guidance
Users see a short message and a reference number; the detail, such as stack traces, database errors and internal paths, goes to the logs. Login and password reset messages should not reveal whether an account exists.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system generates error messages that provide the information needed for corrective action without revealing information that could be exploited. (SI-11a)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall ensure the system reveals detailed error messages only to {{param:si-11_odp}}. (SI-11b)
