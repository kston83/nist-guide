title: 'Minimize personally identifiable information in testing, training and research'
status: draft
stage: operate
typical:
  si-12.02_odp.01: 'de-identified or synthetic data, or where the research needs identifiable data, only the elements the senior privacy official approves'
  si-12.02_odp.02: 'synthetic data, or masked copies of production data'
  si-12.02_odp.03: 'fictitious or synthetic records'
---

:::guidance
Copies of production data in test and training environments are a common source of breaches, because those environments rarely have production's controls. The organization-wide rules and approvals are under PM-25. SI-12(2) is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use the following techniques to minimize the use of personally identifiable information for research: {{param:si-12.02_odp.01}}. (SI-12(2))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use the following techniques to minimize the use of personally identifiable information for testing: {{param:si-12.02_odp.02}}. (SI-12(2))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use the following techniques to minimize the use of personally identifiable information for training: {{param:si-12.02_odp.03}}. (SI-12(2))
