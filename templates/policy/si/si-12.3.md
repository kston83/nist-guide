  si-12.03_odp.01: 'deletion by the system''s automated retention jobs, and shredding for paper output'
  si-12.03_odp.02: 'destruction of storage media by shredding, disintegration or incineration, as the media sanitization procedure sets out'
  si-12.03_odp.03: 'cryptographic erase or overwriting, as the media sanitization procedure sets out'

:::guidance
Disposal at the end of the retention period covers every copy, including backups, exports and copies held by service providers. Sanitizing the media itself is MP-6. SI-12(3) is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use the following techniques to dispose of information following the retention period: {{param:si-12.03_odp.01}}. (SI-12(3))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use the following techniques to destroy information following the retention period: {{param:si-12.03_odp.02}}. (SI-12(3))
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall use the following techniques to erase information following the retention period: {{param:si-12.03_odp.03}}. (SI-12(3))
