:::guidance
Retention applies to everything the system holds or produces: records, logs, backups, reports and exports. Records management usually owns the retention schedule; the system owner makes the system follow it, including deletion at the end of the period. For personally identifiable information, the retention periods also go in the privacy impact assessment.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall manage and retain information within the system, and information output from the system, in accordance with applicable laws, executive orders, directives, regulations, policies, standards, guidelines and operational requirements. (SI-12)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall document the retention period and the authority for it for each type of information the system holds, drawn from the organization's records retention schedule. (SI-12)
- The {{org:system-owner}} shall dispose of information when its retention period ends, unless a legal hold or an approved extension applies. (SI-12)

:::federal
The head of each federal agency must "make and preserve records containing adequate and proper documentation" of the agency's functions ([44 U.S.C. § 3101](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3101&num=0&edition=prelim)), and federal records "may not be alienated or destroyed" except under chapter 33 of title 44 ([44 U.S.C. § 3314](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3314&num=0&edition=prelim)): that is, under records schedules approved by the Archivist of the United States, including the General Records Schedules the Archivist issues for records common to several or all agencies ([44 U.S.C. § 3303a(d)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3303a&num=0&edition=prelim)). As of September 2026.

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall retain and dispose of federal records in the system only as an approved agency records schedule or a General Records Schedule allows. (SI-12)

:::
