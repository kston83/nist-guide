:::guidance
The privacy notice tells individuals how to ask for a correction; this clause makes the system able to carry it out, including in copies shared with others where PM-22 requires notifying them. SI-18(4) is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- For a system that processes personally identifiable information, the {{org:system-owner}} shall correct or delete personally identifiable information upon request by individuals or their designated representatives, once the request is verified and approved. (SI-18(4))
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall ensure each request to correct or delete personally identifiable information is answered, and that a refusal is given in writing with the reason and how to appeal. (SI-18(4))

:::federal
For records in a system of records, the Privacy Act lets an individual request amendment of a record about them. The agency must acknowledge the request in writing "not later than 10 days (excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and legal public holidays)" after receiving it, and then promptly either make the correction or tell the individual of its refusal, the reasons, and how to request a review. A review of a refusal must be completed within 30 days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal public holidays, unless the agency head extends the period for good cause ([5 U.S.C. § 552a(d)(2) and (d)(3)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim)). Subsections (j) and (k) let an agency exempt some systems of records from these provisions by rule. As of September 2026.

- For each system of records, the {{org:privacy-official}} shall acknowledge each request to amend a record in writing within 10 days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal public holidays, and then promptly correct the record or explain the refusal and how to request a review. (SI-18(4))
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall complete the review of a refused amendment within 30 days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal public holidays, unless the agency head extends the period for good cause. (SI-18(4))

:::
