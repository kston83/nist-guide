:::guidance
Checks at collection include validating formats and confirming information with the individual. Periodic checks compare records with authoritative sources and flag records past their retention period. The organization-wide quality policies are under PM-22. SI-18 is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- For a system that processes personally identifiable information, the {{org:system-owner}} shall check the accuracy of the personally identifiable information across the information life cycle {{param:si-18_odp.01}}. (SI-18a)
- For such a system, the {{org:system-owner}} shall check the relevance of the personally identifiable information across the information life cycle {{param:si-18_odp.02}}. (SI-18a)
- For such a system, the {{org:system-owner}} shall check the timeliness of the personally identifiable information across the information life cycle {{param:si-18_odp.03}}. (SI-18a)
- For such a system, the {{org:system-owner}} shall check the completeness of the personally identifiable information across the information life cycle {{param:si-18_odp.04}}. (SI-18a)
- For such a system, the {{org:system-owner}} shall correct or delete inaccurate or outdated personally identifiable information. (SI-18b)

:::federal
The Privacy Act requires each agency that maintains a system of records to maintain all records used in making any determination about an individual "with such accuracy, relevance, timeliness, and completeness as is reasonably necessary to assure fairness to the individual in the determination", and, before disseminating a record to anyone other than an agency (except under subsection (b)(2)), to make reasonable efforts to assure it is accurate, complete, timely and relevant for agency purposes ([5 U.S.C. § 552a(e)(5) and (e)(6)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section552a&num=0&edition=prelim)). As of September 2026.

- For each system of records, the {{org:system-owner}} shall maintain records used in making determinations about individuals with the accuracy, relevance, timeliness and completeness 5 U.S.C. § 552a(e)(5) requires. (SI-18a)
- Before disseminating a record from a system of records to anyone other than an agency, except under 5 U.S.C. § 552a(b)(2), the {{org:system-owner}} shall make reasonable efforts to assure the record is accurate, complete, timely and relevant for agency purposes. (SI-18a)

:::
