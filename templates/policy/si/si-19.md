  si-19_odp.02: 'annually, and before each new release of a de-identified dataset'

:::guidance
Removing direct identifiers is rarely enough: combinations of indirect identifiers, such as postal code, birth date and sex, can re-identify people. Evaluating effectiveness means testing for that risk, especially before a dataset is published. NIST SP 800-188, De-Identifying Government Datasets: Techniques and Governance (September 2023, final as of September 2026), describes the techniques and governance. SI-19 is in the Privacy baseline only.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall remove the following elements of personally identifiable information from datasets that are to be de-identified: {{param:si-19_odp.01}}. (SI-19a)
- The {{org:privacy-official}} shall evaluate the effectiveness of de-identification {{param:si-19_odp.02}}. (SI-19b)
