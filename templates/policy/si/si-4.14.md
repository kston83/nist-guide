:::guidance
A wireless intrusion detection system covers the facilities where the system's components are located, including those with no approved wireless network, since rogue access points are the main concern there.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall employ a wireless intrusion detection system to identify rogue wireless devices and to detect attack attempts and potential compromises or breaches to the system. (SI-4(14))
