---

- The {{org:ciso}} shall train {{param:sr-11.01_odp}} to detect counterfeit system components, including hardware, software and firmware. (SR-11(1))
- The training shall be part of role-based training (AT-3), completed before the person takes on the duties and annually thereafter. (SR-11(1))
- The training shall cover the organization's anti-counterfeit procedures, how to recognize counterfeit or altered hardware, packaging and software, and how to quarantine and report a suspect component. (SR-11(1))

:::guidance
NIST gives no discussion for SR-11(1). SP 800-161 Rev. 1 says the supply chain risk management team can help find training resources and decide who needs the training. Record completions in the training record log, as for other role-based training.
:::
