:::guidance
NIST gives no discussion for SR-11(2). SP 800-161 Rev. 1 says configuration control for component service and repair should be integrated into the organization's configuration control processes, and that component authenticity should be addressed in contracts for servicing and repair. The Maintenance Policy covers approval, sanitization and the check after repair (MA-2); this clause keeps a component under control while it is away and before it returns.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall maintain configuration control over {{param:sr-11.02_odp}} awaiting service or repair, and over serviced or repaired components awaiting return to service. (SR-11(2))
- A component awaiting service or repair, or awaiting return to service, shall be recorded in the component inventory with the status "under maintenance", and kept in a controlled area or in the custody of an authorized person. (SR-11(2))
- A component sent off site shall be tracked by serial number from departure to return, with the maintenance log recording who had custody of it. (SR-11(2))
- Before a serviced or repaired component returns to service, it shall be checked against its inventory record, inspected for tampering (SR-10), and have its firmware and software verified and its secure baseline configuration restored (CM-2, CM-6). (SR-11(2))
- Contracts for component service and repair shall require genuine replacement parts from the original manufacturer or its authorized channel. (SR-11(2))
