:::guidance
NIST's SR-2(1) discussion describes a team-based approach drawing on diverse roles, including the risk executive, information technology, contracting, information security, privacy, mission or business, legal, supply chain and logistics, acquisition and business continuity. It can be an extension of existing security and privacy risk management processes or part of an organizational risk management team. SP 800-161 Rev. 1 calls the central body a C-SCRM program management office. In a small organization the team may be a few people who meet as needed; what matters is that acquisition, security and legal decide together.
:::

- The {{org:ciso}} shall establish a supply chain risk management team consisting of {{param:sr-02.01_odp.01}} to lead and support the following supply chain risk management activities: {{param:sr-02.01_odp.02}}. (SR-2(1))
- The {{org:ciso}} shall document the team's membership, roles, responsibilities and decision authority in a charter. (SR-2(1))
- The team shall meet at least quarterly, and whenever an acquisition or a supplier notification needs its review, and shall record its decisions. (SR-2(1))
