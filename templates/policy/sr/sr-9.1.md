:::guidance
NIST's SR-9(1) discussion lists the life cycle phases, from research and development to disposal, and says organizations use a combination of hardware and software techniques, such as obfuscation and self-checking, to make reverse engineering and modification harder; customizing systems and components can make substitution easier to detect.
:::

- The {{org:system-owner}} shall employ anti-tamper technologies, tools and techniques throughout the system development life cycle: research and development, design, manufacturing, acquisition, delivery, integration, operations and maintenance, and disposal. (SR-9(1))
- Contracts for custom or critical components shall state the anti-tamper requirements the developer or manufacturer must meet in each phase it performs. (SR-9(1))
- The supply chain risk management plan shall record which anti-tamper measures apply in each phase of the life cycle. (SR-9(1))
